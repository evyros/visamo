# Lease or purchase contract (חוזה שכירות או רכישה)

The lease or purchase contract of your home, in both your names: the original, a copy and the lease appendix.

For a couple who live together, or lived together: the lease or purchase
contract of the home you share, or shared. If you've never lived together,
the Israeli partner brings the contract of their own home instead (the lease
or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home).

## What it should include

- Both your names on the contract.
- **If you lived together abroad:** the lease or purchase contract of the
  home you shared there. A contract that isn't in Hebrew or Arabic may need
  a translation (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).
