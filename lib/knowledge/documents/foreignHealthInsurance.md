# Health insurance of the foreign partner (ביטוח בריאות של בן/בת הזוג הזר/ה)

Health insurance for the foreign partner in Israel: until A/5, they have no public health insurance. A private Israeli plan for non-residents, or a policy from abroad that covers Israel.

While the foreign partner holds a B/1 visa, they aren't a resident for
National Insurance, so they have no public health insurance: that starts with
the A/5. Until then, they need private health insurance, and the office may
ask to see it. Whatever the plan, through an employer, a health fund or an
insurance company, on a B/1 it's always private insurance.

## How to get it

- **From a health fund (קופת חולים), for non-residents:** for example
  Maccabi's plan for non-residents, or Meuhedet's plan for external members.
- **A partner's plan:** Clalit (פוליסת נשוי לתושב) and Meuhedet offer a cheaper
  plan to the partner of an Israeli who is a member of that fund, married or
  not. You join together at the Israeli partner's branch, with a health
  declaration, and proof of your relationship in Hebrew: a marriage
  certificate with a notarized translation, the Israeli partner's appendix
  (ספח) listing the spouse, or an affidavit, from an Israeli lawyer, that
  you're married or common-law partners. These plans have a minimum period of a
  few months, and Meuhedet's cheaper rate has an age limit.
- **From an insurance company in Israel:** health plans for foreigners and
  tourists, with the treatment usually given through one of the health funds.
- **From abroad:** a travel or health policy that covers Israel. It's usually
  less convenient: you often pay for treatment yourself and claim it back.
- **Through work:** an employer may have to insure a worker on a B/1 with a
  foreign worker's health insurance. Not every employer applies it to
  partners of Israelis, but it's worth asking for.

Plans differ in what they cover and exclude: check that it covers what you
need.

## What it should include

- **The foreign partner's name** as the insured person.
- **The insurer, and the coverage period:** from when to when it's valid.
- **Coverage in Israel.**
- **A policy from abroad:** a certified translation unless it's in Hebrew or
  Arabic. In practice, English is usually accepted without one (see
  [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## Questions

**My partner is still abroad. Do they need it now?**
They need it for their stay in Israel. Arrange it for when they arrive.

**Can my partner join my health fund?**
Not as a member of the public health insurance while on a B/1. But Clalit and
Meuhedet offer a private partner's plan to the partner of a member.
