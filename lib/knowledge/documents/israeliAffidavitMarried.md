# Affidavit of the Israeli partner (תצהיר של בן/בת הזוג הישראלי/ת)

A sworn statement in the ministry's wording, that the documents are true and that you'll report any change. Signed in front of a lawyer.

The Israeli partner's declaration, titled **הצהרת בן הזוג המזמין**
("declaration of the inviting partner"), is part of form AS/6, the status
application for a foreign partner married to an Israeli (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ז).
It's usually page 3 or 4 of the form (נספח אש6). In it, the Israeli partner declares
a list of statements about the marriage, and that everything they submit is
true.

## How to get it

The form is part of AS/6, on the Population and Immigration Authority's site
(see [sources.md](../sources.md)). It's in Hebrew, so it needs no translation.

1. The Israeli partner ticks (☒) the box next to each statement they
   declare.
2. They write the place and the date, and sign it **in front of a lawyer
   (עו"ד) or a Misrad Hapnim registrar (רשם)**, who confirms the declaration
   at the bottom of the page.

## What it should include

- **A tick next to each statement**. By ticking, the Israeli partner
  declares that:
  - the marriage is real, and wasn't made to get status in Israel;
  - they aren't registered as married (other than to their partner) in the
    population register of any country;
  - they undertake to take in the foreign partner, and to cover the costs of
    their stay, housing and living in Israel, without the foreign partner
    needing help from the authorities in Israel or any other body, subject
    to the law;
  - the foreign partner is their only spouse, and they're married only to
    them;
  - the foreign partner isn't married to anyone else, and they're the
    foreign partner's only spouse.
- **An explanation for any statement they can't declare**, in the lines
  under the list, with the details or the reason.
- **The place, the date and the Israeli partner's signature.**
- **The lawyer's or registrar's confirmation** (אישור קבלת ההצהרה): their
  name and license number, the Israeli partner's name and the ID it was
  checked against, a statement that the Israeli partner was warned to tell
  the truth and confirmed the declaration in front of them, and their stamp
  and signature.

The application number box (מספר בקשה) at the top can stay empty if you
don't have a number yet.

## Questions

**What if I can't tick one of the statements?**
Don't tick it. Write the details or the reason in the lines under the list
("אם אינך יכול/ה להצהיר…").

**What happens if something in it isn't true?**
The declaration says that false or incomplete details, including details
from the documents you submit, can lead to cancelling any visa given, and
to a removal order from Israel against the foreign partner (sections 11 and
13 of the Entry into Israel Law, 1952). Giving false details or a forged
document is also a criminal offense.

**The form is written in the masculine. Does it apply to me?**
Yes. The form says the masculine wording is only for convenience, and it's
meant for women and men alike.

**Does it have to be in front of a lawyer?**
A lawyer, or a Misrad Hapnim registrar: the confirmation at the bottom of the
form can be signed by either. It is a requirement.
