# Foreign passport (דרכון זר)

Valid for at least 2 more years, with copies of the main page, the visa pages and the border-control stamps.

The foreign passport, valid for at least 2 more years (§ד.2.ד), with copies
of the main page, **the visa pages and the border-control stamps**.

If the foreign partner is outside Israel, a copy is enough for the first
appointment. They bring the passport itself after they arrive, when the
office gives them the visa.
