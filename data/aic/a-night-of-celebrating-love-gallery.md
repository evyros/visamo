# A Night of Celebrating Love… [Gallery]

After months of eager enquiries about when our next event would be, we couldn’t miss out on the opportunity to celebrate February – the Month of Love! On the evening of the 22nd of February, more than 80 people, all international couples, gathered together in central Tel Aviv for our event, *Celebrating Love*. The night was filled with delicious food, toasts, laughter, and a beautiful slideshow of our gorgeous international couples…

Of course, it also wouldn’t have been complete without our International Couples Bingo, where many couples shared hilarious, mind-blowing and bittersweet stories of their experiences as international couples living in Israel. Congratulations to Eve, who won the Bingo by finding 25 couples with very special stories and unique attributes! Eve won two theatre tickets, for [a play accompanied by English-subtitles](https://aic.org.il/events/), courtesy of Beit Lessin Theatre.

A huge shout-out to AIC’s Events Team, led by Adi Shalev, and all the fantastic AIC volunteers who made this event possible! Big thanks to Fredrick Simon for snapping some awesome photos of our community during the evening – check them out in the gallery below!

This event was subsidized by AIC and made possible by our amazing team of volunteers who dedicate their own free time for the community! Did you enjoy the event? Looking forward for future ones? We can’t do it without your support >>> [Please donate to AIC today](https://aic.org.il/donations/)!

Missed the event or couldn’t make it? Our next community event is **coming up next month**! [Subscribe to our newsletter](https://aic.org.il/subscription-to-newsletter/) to get notified first and never miss a great time again!
