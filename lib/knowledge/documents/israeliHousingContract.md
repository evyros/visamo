# Lease or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home (חוזה שכירות או רכישה של הדירה של בן/בת הזוג הישראלי/ת)

The lease or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home, in their name: the original, a copy and the lease appendix.

For a couple who have never lived together, when the Israeli partner lives
in Israel: the lease of the Israeli partner's own home, in their name, with
all its appendices. If the Israeli partner lives abroad too, it isn't asked
for: see what to bring instead in [documents.md](../documents.md).

## What it should include

- **The Israeli partner's name on the lease.**
- **All its appendices**, with the original and a copy.
- **If the Israeli partner owns the home:** the purchase contract of the
  property, instead of a lease.
