# Official Documentation from the Netherlands

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from the Netherlands

Name in The Netherlands: Geboorteakte

**How to obtain?**

Request from the municipality where you were born. Most will provide an international version (includes English). You can easily add an apostille by visiting one of the country’s courts (Rechtbank).

**Cost: ~15 EUR**

**Link:** [**City of Amsterdam**](https://www.amsterdam.nl/veelgevraagd/?productid=%7BFCCA0186-543A-4EB2-89EA-59EB5A6929FB%7D#case_%7B8E93049D-3815-4611-ADFD-BFE2CFA25397%7D) (as example)

## Name change certificate from the Netherlands

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in The Netherlands: Uittreksel voor/achternaamswijziging

**How to obtain?**

Received from Justis if you’ve gone through this process. If you’ve changed your name due to marriage, your original name is still visible on your registry in the municipality (uittreksel bevolkingsregister); you could use that document to demonstrate your original name to the embassy if they need this information.

**Cost:** —

**Link:** [**Justis**](https://justis.nl/producten/naamswijziging/aanvraagprocedure-naamswijziging)

## Personal status certificate from the Netherlands

This document does not exist in the Netherlands, it cannot be requested from official authorities.

Instead, you can either 1) provide a statement at the Israeli Embassy in The Hague and get the document there, or, 2) go to a lawyer/notary for a formal statement/affidavit.

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from the Netherlands

Name in The Netherlands: Verklaring Omtrent Gedrag

**How to obtain?**

This is a bit tricky in The Netherlands, because typically a VOG needs to be requested by the authority that needs it (e.g. an employer or an embassy). Since the Israeli Embassy will not request it for you, you will need to request it yourself. This can be done by going to the [VOG website](https://justis.nl/producten/verklaring-omtrent-het-gedrag-vog/aanvragen), downloading the form and filling it out. The document will not be signed by the embassy, but you will instead need to attach a copy of [this page](https://embassies.gov.il/hague-en/ConsularServices/Pages/local-visa-information.aspx) of the Israeli website which officially indicates that you need the VOG for this request. You will then need to send it by regular mail, along with proof of payment and a copy of your passport. You can add an apostille by going to any court.  

**Cost:** ~34 EUR

**Link:** [**VOG website**](https://justis.nl/producten/verklaring-omtrent-het-gedrag-vog/aanvragen)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in the Netherlands

**How to obtain?**

Go to any court and take the document for which you need an apostille with you. You don’t need an appointment, you can walk in, get a number and get the document straight away. It typically doesn’t take long. Bring identification with you.

**Cost: ~30 EUR per document**

**Link**: **[Rechtbank](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/Onderwerpen/apostille-legalisatie#:~:text=Voor%20het%20aanvragen%20van%20een,u%20het%20griffierecht%20heeft%20betaald.)**
