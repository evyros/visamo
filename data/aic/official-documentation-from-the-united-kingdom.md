# Official Documentation from the United Kingdom

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from the UK

Name in UK: Birth Certificate

**How to obtain?**

Using the gov.uk website (which is brilliant);

You can get help from the General Register Office too – tel: 03001231837.

**Cost: £11 or £35** if you need it quickly.

**[Link](https://www.gov.uk/order-copy-birth-death-marriage-certificate)**

## Name change certificate from the UK

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in UK: Enrolled Deed Poll

**How to obtain?**

Applying to the Royal Courts of Justice to have your name change put on public record.

You can contact UK Deed Poll for help. Tel: 02080172216 email: [helpline@ukdp.co.uk](mailto:helpline@ukdp.co.uk)

Name changes are also published on the [Official Public Record](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/all-notices/notice?categorycode=G406000002&location-distance-1=1&numberOfLocationSearches=1&results-page-size=10).

**Cost: £42.44**

[**Link**](https://www.gov.uk/change-name-deed-poll/enrol-a-deed-poll-with-the-courts)

## Personal status certificate from the UK

Name in UK: Certificate of No Impediment

**How to obtain?**

It’s a strange one.

In the UK this document can only be obtained by saying that you are getting married abroad, and it states that there is “no impediment” to your marriage, hence you are single. You need to contact your local council and tell them that you are getting married abroad and you need this certificate, and you will have to supply them with info about the “wedding” (date and venue) and yours and your partner’s names. Yours and your partner’s names are then written on a sheet of paper that is on the wall of your local council for a whole month – the idea is that this will give people a chance to publicly come forward and say there is a reason you cannot marry (there is an ”impediment”).

After a month has passed, you will be sent the document declaring your status as a single person who is able to get married. This is the only UK document that declares a person as single and is quite bizarre. Remember you need to give it more than a month and there is no way to do it any shorter so you should start this process ASAP.

**[Link](https://www.gov.uk/marriage-abroad)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from the UK

Name in UK: Police Certificate

**How to obtain?**

By filling in the form online on the ACRO website and supplying them with all the documents they need (proof of address, photograph, passport/ID, national insurance or driving license number, your address history for the past 10 years).

Do not get this document confused with a DBS/CRC check; these are different. For the Israeli visa you need the Police Certificate through ACRO.

**[Link](https://www.acro.police.uk/Police-Certificates-Online)**

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in the UK

**How to obtain?**

Again, using the gov.uk website, they talk you through the process. You need to send the original document to them, having printed off the postage label that they will supply you with once you have paid online (it costs £30 per document). Therefore you need access to a printer.  They send the document(s) back to you with the apostilles attached within two weeks. You can pay £75 if you need it urgently.

You can contact the legalization office for help – tel: 03700002244

**[Link](https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised)**
