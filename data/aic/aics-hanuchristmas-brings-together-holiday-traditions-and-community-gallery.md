# AIC’s HanuChristmas Brings Together Holiday Traditions and Community [Gallery]

On December 18th, on the fifth night of Hanukkah, we brought our community together for a festive **HanuChristmas** evening, blending the spirit of Hanukkah and Christmas into one warm, lively winter celebration! With holiday-chic outfits, twinkling décor, and great music, the atmosphere felt like a true seasonal gathering… Israeli style, with an international twist!

Throughout the night, couples joined in decorating our shared Christmas tree, lighting the fifth Hanukkah candle, raising a holiday toast, and enjoying light games that brought plenty of laughter and connection. Guests also enjoyed a delicious buffet-style spread, with AIC providing all the food and drinks, keeping everyone happily fueled for a fun holiday night. The mix of traditions created a cozy, joyful atmosphere where new friendships formed and familiar faces reconnected.

And of course, no AIC event is complete without photo magnets and a sufganiya to sweeten the evening. You can explore some of the highlights from the celebration in our **photo gallery below.**

We thank AIC’s events team for all their hard work and dedication. With a minimal event donation, and thanks to huge volunteering efforts, AIC was able to create an amazing event. To allow us to continue hosting events and fostering bonds within our community, [please donate to AIC today](https://aic.org.il/donations/)!

Missed the event or couldn’t make it? [Subscribe to our newsletter](https://aic.org.il/subscription-to-newsletter/) to get notified first and never miss a great time again!

See you at the next event!
