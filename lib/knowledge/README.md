# The Israeli partner visa: what you need to know

A guide to getting status in Israel for the foreign partner of an Israeli,
through the gradual process (ההליך המדורג).

- [process.md](process.md): what the process is, how long it takes and how long each wait takes, and who it's for.
- [application.md](application.md): filing the application online, and what happens while you wait.
- [first-appointment.md](first-appointment.md): the first appointment at Misrad Hapnim, and the first visa.
- [entry-permit.md](entry-permit.md): the entry permit a partner abroad needs to come and start the process, and the two ways to get it.
- [visiting.md](visiting.md): a visit only, as a tourist, without starting the process: showing it's a visit, a refusal at the border, and the tourist invitation.
- [interview.md](interview.md): the interview, and how to prepare for it.
- [deposit.md](deposit.md): the deposit (ערבות) the office sometimes asks for, how to pay it, and how to get it back.
- [both-abroad.md](both-abroad.md): when you both live abroad, starting at the Israeli consulate.
- [documents.md](documents.md): the documents for the first application, each one described in [documents/](documents/).
- [certification.md](certification.md): apostille, legalization, translation and affidavits.
- [affidavits.md](affidavits.md): how to make an affidavit in Israel, and what it costs.
- [children.md](children.md): when the foreign partner's children move to Israel.
- [pregnancy.md](pregnancy.md): a pregnancy during the process: the baby's registration, recognizing the father, and the cost of the birth.
- [former-ussr.md](former-ussr.md): the former USSR, and the Nativ step.
- [security-check.md](security-check.md): the security check, only for Palestinian residents and citizens of Arab countries.
- [sources.md](sources.md): where this knowledge comes from, and the official procedures and forms.
- [glossary.md](glossary.md): the words you'll meet, in English and Hebrew.

Where the law and how offices work in practice differ, both are given.
