# Where these files come from

The form is the Population and Immigration Authority's form AS/1, from
https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS1.pdf, at 200 dpi,
filled in for the eval's couple (Noa Levi inviting John Smith, made up).

The as1-handwritten files are the same, written by hand and photographed (a
handwriting font, turned and shaded as a phone photo of each page), with B/2
as the visa category. The b1 set has B/1, and is messier: smaller, uneven,
smudged and slightly out of focus.
