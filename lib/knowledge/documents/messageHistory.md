# Message and call history (היסטוריית הודעות ושיחות)

Up to 10 pages of your messages (WhatsApp, email, social media) or call logs, from different times in your relationship.

Your history of messages and calls with each other is part of the evidence
that your relationship is genuine and ongoing. It's on the lists couples are
asked for at the first application, and it matters most when you haven't
lived together, or have spent long periods apart.

## How to get it

From your phones and accounts: screenshots of your chats (WhatsApp, Telegram,
Messenger, Instagram and the like), printouts of emails, or your call log.
Print them, or save them as one PDF for the online application.

## What it should include

- **Up to about 10 pages.** Choose, don't print everything.
- **From different times** in your relationship, from early on to recently,
  not one day.
- **The dates visible** on the messages or calls.
- **Both of you visible** as the two sides of the conversation.
- **If it isn't in Hebrew or English:** you don't need a notarized translation.
  Translate the parts that matter yourselves, or mark them and explain them,
  so the clerk can follow (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## Questions

**Do we need to translate our chats?**
Not with a notary. If they aren't in Hebrew or English, translate or explain
the important parts yourselves.

**Can we send our whole chat history?**
No. Choose up to about 10 pages that show your relationship over time.
