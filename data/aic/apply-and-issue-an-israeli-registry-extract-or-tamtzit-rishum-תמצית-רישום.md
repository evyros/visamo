# Apply and Issue an Israeli Registry Extract / “Tamtzit Rishum”

An Israeli **Registry Extract** (“**תמצית רישום**“) is a document, issued by the Population and Immigration Authority, in which they state all of the registered data about Israeli citizens or residents.

The Tamtzit Rishum is frequently used to prove a person’s single status, for example, before marrying abroad. It’s important to understand that Israel does not issue a specific “single status certificate” or a “certificate of non-impediment” (“nulla osta”), which many EU countries require for marriage. However, countries like Cyprus, South Africa, and Sweden, among others, accept the Israeli Tamtzit Rishum as sufficient proof of availability to be married.

Issuing a Tamtzit Rishum is a **free-of-charge** service**.**

## Who can apply for a Registry Extract?

The issuing of a Registry Extract, known as the “Tamtzit Rishum,” is available to Israeli residents and citizens currently in Israel. Citizens residing abroad who wish to obtain this service should contact the Israeli embassy in their country of residence.

## How to Get a Tamtzit Rishum?

### Digital Tamtzit Rishum – Online Direct Download

The fastest and easiest way to obtain a Tamtzit Rishum is by downloading a digitally signed certificate through GovIL, the government’s personal online portal.

- Go to [GovIL](https://www.gov.il/) and log in to your personal area (you must sign up to the National Identification System if you haven’t before).
- Under the “Documents” section, find your registry extract and that of your minor children.
- Download a digitally-signed certificate to your devices.
- If you need to use the Tamtzit Rishum abroad – choose “תמצית רישום מורחבת”, which can be downloaded with a digital apostille.

Three additional ways to apply and get a Tamtzit Rishum (other than for special cases as listed below that require in-person presence):

### Online Request for Physical Certificate

A Population Registry Extract for Israelis can be issued at no cost by filing an [online request](https://www.gov.il/he/service/summaryinfofromthepopulationregistrylisting). After the request is submitted, the certificate is mailed to the address registered on the person’s identity card. It takes about 2-3 weeks for the certificate to arrive by mail.

In the online form, you will be able to pick additional information that can appear in the Registry Extract such as names of parents, city of birth, names of children and/or spouse, previous names, previous addresses, etc.

### Self Service Kiosks

Another way to issue the Registry Extract is via one of PIBA’s self-service kiosks or one of the government services kiosks around Israel. This service is only available to Israeli citizens/residents who **have a biometric ID**. For verification purposes, the applicant must have their personal PIN or a personal credit card when applying at the kiosk.

- [List of PIBA offices that have self-service kiosks](https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/general/slf_service_stands)
- [List of governmental services kiosks that support the issuance of Tamtzit Rishum](https://www.gov.il/he/government-service-branches/?officeId=81118354-e792-4e08-9057-f7ae5200cbf9&categories=c81d766b-3e23-4696-a43e-03ffbf317c64&limit=10&categoryId=c81d766b-3e23-4696-a43e-03ffbf317c64) – much larger availability.

**The Registry Extract will be printed and delivered on the spot.**

### Apply In Person at PIBA Office

You can apply for a Tamzit Rishum in person at PIBA offices. For that, you can just walk in during the opening hours of the Mirsham department and queue up by taking a number. A Tamtzit Rishum application does not require a scheduled appointment. You must have your Israeli ID with you. Driver’s License is not enough!

You should print and fill up [Form MR2](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/summaryinfofromthepopulationregistrylisting/he/MR2.pdf) which you will have to submit to the clerk. You can also find the form at the office and fill it out on the spot.

The application form for the registration summary must be filled out online and sent.

When submitting an application in person at the office, **the Registry Extract will be printed and delivered on the spot.**

When applying in person, it is important to indicate in the application form the reason for the application and whether it is necessary that the extract will include certain information, such as previous addresses or family members.

### Application by Third Parties

If you are applying for other family members or non-family members, a power of attorney approved by a lawyer or a court ruling must be attached and the request must be submitted in person only.

An application for a registry extract for a deceased person who is a first-degree relative or who has an interest in the matter, the request must be submitted in person only.

In a request for an extended registration summary for a deceased person, a court order must be attached and the request must be submitted in person only.
