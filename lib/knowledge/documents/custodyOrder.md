# Court ruling on custody or guardianship (פסק דין על משמורת או אפוטרופסות)

The original, approved by a court: a ruling or a divorce agreement giving you sole custody or guardianship of the children, or a ruling allowing you to apply for them without the other parent's consent. With a custody ruling, also proof the children actually live with you.

Without the other parent's consent, one of these, the original and approved
by a court (AS/6, in the application, under the children coming along; §ה.2(9)י):

- a court ruling giving you sole custody or guardianship of the children;
- a divorce agreement approved by a court, giving you sole custody or
  guardianship;
- a court ruling allowing you to apply for the children's status without the
  other parent's consent.

With a custody ruling, also **proof the children actually live with you**
(§ה.2(9)י).
