# Where these files come from

philippines-real-death-apostille.jpg and philippines-real-death-certificate.jpg
are a real file, shared by the app's owner, as screenshots: the PSA death
certificate of the foreign partner's first husband (died 5 May 2024), with
the Department of Foreign Affairs' electronic apostille (3 September 2026).
The same files as foreignCivilStatus's, and the same woman as
marriageCertificateAbroad's and foreignBirthCertificate's Philippine files.
For Israel, the Philippines issues them only as files.
