# The process

## What it is

A foreign partner of an Israeli gets status through the **graduated
procedure** (ההליך המדורג): a few years of temporary visas before permanent
status. The foreign partner usually moves from a B/1 visa (with a work
permit) to A/5 (temporary resident), and at the end to citizenship or
permanent residency. Each stage is renewed with updated documents.

- **Married couples:** procedure 5.2.0008.
- **Common-law couples** (ידועים בציבור), including same-sex couples:
  procedure 5.2.0009. It needs more proof that you live together, and proof
  that you're both free to marry.

## Who can sponsor

- **An Israeli citizen.** The foreign partner ends the process with
  citizenship.
- **A permanent resident** (תושב קבע). The foreign partner ends with permanent
  residency, not citizenship. The check that your life is centered in Israel
  is usually stricter.
- **A temporary resident (A/5)** generally can't sponsor a partner.

## Who this process isn't for

- **Residents of the Palestinian territories, and citizens of Iran, Iraq,
  Syria and Lebanon**, fall under the Citizenship and Entry into Israel Law.
  Most can't use the graduated procedure: 5.2.0008 §ה.2(1) rejects them at
  the start (for residents of the territories, by age: men under 35, women
  under 25).
- **A Jewish foreign partner, or one with a Jewish parent or grandparent**,
  may be able to immigrate under the Law of Return instead, a different and
  faster route.

## Good to know

- If the Israeli partner sponsored a foreign partner before, the application
  is looked at more closely. It doesn't change the documents you need.
