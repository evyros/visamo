# Official Documentation from Argentina

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Argentina

Name in Argentina: Partida de Nacimiento

**How to obtain?**

Case A) If you were born in Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires (CABA) → you go to the link below, register/log into your miBA account and fill out the form. It’s actually really quick and they send it to you by email in a few days.

Case B) Provincia de Buenos Aires (PBA) → go to the link below, register/log into your account and fill the form. You can then see and download the birth certificate from the site.   
Case C) Other provinces→ It changes in every province, if you google “Partida de Nacimiento X province, Argentina”, you’ll find the information. Always check if you’re in the original site from your province and see if the site is secure.

**Cost: Case A) CABA→ $645-$1380 (Pesos Argentinos)**. It depends on how much information you provide and how soon you need it.  
**Case B) PBA→ $580-$2300 (Pesos Argentinos)**.  
  
**Link:** **[Case A) CABA](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/solicitud-de-partidas), [Case B) PBA](https://www.gba.gob.ar/registrodelaspersonas/solicitud_de_partidas_online)**

Case C) Other→Write on Google ““Partida de Nacimiento X province, Argentina” and you’ll find the link needed.

## Personal status certificate from Argentina

Name in Argentina: Certificado de Soltería/Constancia Negativa de Soltería

**How to obtain?**

Case A) Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires (CABA) → You have to make an appointment online and go in person.

Case B) Provincia de Buenos Aires (PBA) → You have to make an appointment online and go in person.

**Cost:  Case A) CABA→ $200 (Pesos Argentinos)**

**Case B) PBA→ $389-$1167 (Pesos Argentinos)**

**Link: [Case A) CABA](https://www.buenosaires.gob.ar/tramites/certificado-de-solteria#:~:text=Es%20el%20tr%C3%A1mite%20a%20trav%C3%A9s,la%20Ciudad%20de%20Buenos%20Aires), [Case B) PBA](https://gba.gob.ar/registrodelaspersonas/constancia_negativa_de_matrimonio_solteria)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Argentina

Name in Argentina: Certificado de Antecedentes Penales

**How to obtain?**

Go to the website, fill out the form, pay online and then you get the certificate sent to your email.

**Cost:** $650-$980 (Pesos Argentinos).

[**Link**](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/reincidencia/antecedentespenales)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Argentina

**How to obtain?**

Case A) Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires→ If you live or can get there, an easy way to get the apostille is make an appointment online at the Colegio de Escribanos, you go there on the set date, hand in the documents and they’ll tell you when to come back. It’s more expensive than the 2nd option, but it’s usually faster.  
Case B) Every city/province in Argentina (Including CABA)→ Go to the link below and follow the steps.

**Cost: Case A) CABA→ $3400 (Pesos Argentinos)  
Case B) Every city→ $300 (Pesos Argentinos)**

**Link**: [Case A) CABA](https://www.colegio-escribanos.org.ar/index.php/legalizaciones/), [Case B) Every city](https://www.cancilleria.gob.ar/es/servicios/servicios/apostilla-legalizacion-con-validez-internacional-tad) // [Link](https://tramitesadistancia.gob.ar/#/inicio)
