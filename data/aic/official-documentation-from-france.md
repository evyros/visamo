# Official Documentation from France

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain the official “Threshold Documents”, needed to apply for the gradual process, and how to verify them. These documents are official documentation about the foreign partner, from their home country, and they are one of the basic requirements of the gradual process. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the gradual process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from France

Name in France: Acte de naissance (copie intégrale)

**How to obtain?**

You request it from the municipality of your birthplace (Mairie de votre lieu de naissance) by phone or via its website depending on the municipality. It usually takes up to a week to have it sent to you by mail, or if you/a parent can physically go there you might also get it on the spot.

## Name change certificate from France

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

No information.

## Personal status certificate from France

Name in France: Acte de Célibat ou Acte d’Etat Civil

**How to obtain?**

A bit tricky because this document doesn’t really exist in France anymore and is quite uncommon. You also request it  from the municipality of your birthplace (Mairie de votre lieu de naissance) by phone or via its website depending on the municipality. It’s a good idea to ask for both this document and the Acte de Naissance at the same time.

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from France

Name in France: Extrait de casier judiciaire

**How to obtain?**

You request it online and it is sent by mail within 1-2 weeks.

[**Link**](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1420)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in France

**How to obtain?**

**Acte de Naissance and Acte de Célibat**: you get the apostille at the Cour d’Appel of your city.  You need to bring/send to the court:

1\. Request form

2\. Document you want the apostille for

3\. A stamped envelope for the Cour to send it back to you within 1-2 weeks.

A family member can do it for you. In the link below you have the request form to fill out and the contact details of the Cour d’Appel of your city. It’s worth calling the Cour to see if someone can go physically there and get the apostille on the spot (or at least give the documents).

[**Link**](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R47867)

**Extrait de casier judiciaire**: It’s the same process as the other apostille, but no matter where you live in France, the apostille is only done in the Cour d’Appel in Rennes.
