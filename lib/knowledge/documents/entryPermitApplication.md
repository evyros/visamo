# Entry permit application (form AS/1) (בקשה להיתר כניסה (טופס אש/1))

Needed while the foreign partner is outside Israel, so they can enter for the process.

Form **AS/1** (אש/1), "Application for entry visa to Israel" (בקשה למתן אשרת
כניסה לישראל), is the entry permit application, for a foreign partner who is
outside Israel (5.2.0008 §ד.2.א). The Israeli partner applies as the inviter
(המזמין), for the foreign partner, the invitee (המוזמן). It's a general form,
also used by employers and institutions, so some fields (such as the company
name) don't apply to you. It has two pages, in Hebrew and English.

## How to get it

The form is on the Population and Immigration Authority's site
(https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS1.pdf).

1. Fill in every detail on the form: the form's instructions ask for all of
   them. The foreign partner's names and addresses abroad are filled in **in
   English**, where the form says so.
2. Attach an explanatory letter and supporting documents: the form's
   instructions ask for them.
3. Each of you signs your own declaration on page 2 (below).

## What it should include

### Page 1

- **The visa you're asking for**, at the top of the form: "Application for
  entry visa to Israel category of ___" (בקשה למתן אשרת כניסה לישראל מסוג).
  It's required. The type depends on the purpose of entry; couples usually
  ask for **B/1 (ב/1)**, so the foreign partner can stay and work in Israel.
  Write the same type at the top of page 2.
- **The Israeli partner's details** (פרטי המזמין הישראלי): first and family
  name, ID number (ת.ז.), relation to the invitee, and cellphone number. The
  company name and company ID are for employers.
- **The foreign partner's details** (פרטי המוזמן), in English: family name,
  given name, father's name and mother's name. Then the mother's maiden
  name, any previous family name, nationality, religion, country and date of
  birth, citizenship and occupation.
- **Family status**: single, married, divorced or widowed.
- **The travel document**: a passport or a laissez-passer, with its number,
  where it was issued, and until when it's valid.
- **Gender.**
- **The foreign partner's spouse** (בן/בת הזוג של המוזמן): family name, given
  name, maiden name, father's name, place and date of birth.
- **The foreign partner's children** (ילדים של המוזמן), up to three on the
  form: given name, country and date of birth.
- **The purpose of entry into Israel.**
- **Previous stays in Israel**: any status held, with dates, an Israeli ID
  number if they had one, a previous foreign passport number, and whether a
  status in Israel was ever cancelled, and why.
- **Earlier requests**: whether a request to visit Israel, the Palestinian
  Authority or the Gaza Strip was ever rejected, and whether one is being
  filed together with this one. For each "yes": when and where it was filed.

### Page 2

- **The foreign partner's details again**, in English: family name, given
  name, passport number and citizenship.
- **Their permanent address abroad**, in English: country, town, street and
  house number, and phone number.
- **Their address in Israel**: town, street and house number, and their
  email address.
- **The Israeli embassy or consulate** to tell when the permit is approved.
- **The Israeli partner's declaration**, with the place, date and their
  signature. They declare that the details are true. They also undertake:
  - not to employ the foreign partner after they enter Israel, for the
    permit's period;
  - that the foreign partner, and anyone coming with them, will leave Israel
    when the permit ends;
  - to pay the authority any expenses of sending them back abroad, if
    needed.

  In it, they also mark whether the foreign partner will or won't receive a
  salary or payment in Israel.
- **The foreign partner's declaration**, with the place, date and their
  signature. They declare that:
  - the details are true;
  - they haven't committed a criminal offense, and haven't acted against
    the Jewish people or the security of the State of Israel;
  - there's no pending legal case or arrest warrant against them, and they
    aren't wanted by the police of any country;
  - they've had no removal order from Israel, and no refusal of entry.

  They also undertake to stay in Israel by the permit's terms, and to leave
  when it ends.
- **"For official use only"** (לשימוש המשרד) is filled in by the office:
  leave it empty.

## Questions

**Where do I send it, or who do I give it to?**
You submit it together with your first application, with the rest of its
documents. It isn't sent separately.

**Which visa do we ask for?**
Usually B/1 (ב/1), which lets the foreign partner stay and work in Israel.
The type depends on the purpose of entry, and you write it at the top of the
form. It's a required field.

**Who signs the form?**
Both of you, each on your own declaration on page 2: the Israeli partner as
the inviter, the foreign partner as the invitee.

**Do we fill in the company fields?**
No. The company name and company ID are for an employer or institution
inviting someone. As a couple, the Israeli partner fills in their own name
and ID number.

**Which parts are in English?**
The foreign partner's names (on both pages) and their address abroad: the
form marks them "In English".
