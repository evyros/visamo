# Divorce decree (פסק דין לגירושין)

The final divorce decree of each of the Israeli partner's previous marriages that ended in divorce.

For each of the Israeli partner's previous marriages that ended in divorce:
the final divorce decree, or the divorce certificate, which shows the
divorce is complete. The AS/6 checklist doesn't list it for the Israeli
partner, but in practice offices ask for it.

## How to get it

### A divorce in Israel

It comes from the court that granted it, depending on your religion:

- **Jewish couples:** the rabbinical court (בית הדין הרבני) issues a divorce
  certificate (תעודת גירושין). If you lost it, ask the rabbinical court for
  another copy, for a fee: you can ask by email.
- **Muslim couples:** the Sharia court (בית הדין השרעי).
- **Christian couples:** the church's ecclesiastical court (בית הדין
  האקלזיאסטי).
- **Couples without a shared recognized religion:** the family court (בית
  המשפט לענייני משפחה), with its judgment dissolving the marriage (התרת
  נישואין).

It's an Israeli document, in Hebrew: no certification or translation.

### A divorce abroad

It comes from the court or authority that granted the divorce, in that
country, like the foreign partner's divorce decree, and the same documents
apply (see the foreign partner's divorce decree, by country). It's certified
there: an apostille, or consular legalization if that country isn't in the
Apostille Convention (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## What it should include

- **One for each divorce.**
- **The final decree or certificate:** the document that ends the marriage,
  not a step on the way.
- **For a divorce abroad:** the certification of that country, and a
  certified translation unless it's in Hebrew or Arabic. In practice, an
  English document is usually accepted without one.

## Questions

**I lost my divorce certificate from the rabbinical court. What now?**
Ask the rabbinical court for another copy. It's issued for a fee, and you
can ask by email.

**I was divorced more than once. Do I bring all of them?**
Yes: a decree or certificate for each previous marriage that ended in
divorce.
