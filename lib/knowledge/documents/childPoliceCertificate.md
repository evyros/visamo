# Children's police certificates (תעודות יושר של הילדים)

A certificate of no criminal record for each child moving to Israel who is 14 or older.

A police certificate for each child moving to Israel aged 14 or older
(§ד.2.י).

## After you get it

In practice, from the day the child's police certificate is issued until
they get their visa, they shouldn't travel to another country. Misrad
Hapnim knows every exit from Israel and every entry into it, and a trip
abroad after the certificate was issued is time it doesn't cover. If they
did travel, they need a new police certificate from their country of
origin.

## Questions

**Can the child travel abroad after getting the police certificate?**
In practice, not until they have their visa: a trip after it's issued is
time it doesn't cover (see above).

**The child already traveled abroad after getting it. What now?**
They need a new police certificate from their country of origin.
