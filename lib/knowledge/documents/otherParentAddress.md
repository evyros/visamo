# The other parent's current address (הכתובת העדכנית של ההורה השני)

An official document with the other parent's current address. The ministry writes to them to ask if they object to the move. Not needed if they live in Israel.

When the other parent is alive and has parental rights: an official document
with their current address, together with their consent. The ministry also
contacts the other parent itself: through the Israeli consulate if they live
abroad, or by registered mail if they live in Israel (then no address
document is needed). See [children.md](../children.md).
