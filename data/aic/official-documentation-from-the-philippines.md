# Official Documentation from The Philippines

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from The Philippines

Name in Philippines: PSA Birth Certificate (also NSO Birth Certificate)

**How to obtain?**

Birth certificate can be obtained from the Philippine Statistics office (PSA) in-person or online. 

1. In-person Request – The document can be requested personally from official PSA branch offices or accredited centers (in SM Business Centers). A scheduled appointment is required. To set an appointment, select the PSA outlet or branch then provide the necessary personal information and preferred appointment schedule. You should receive an appointment slip via email. On the day of your appointment, bring the appointment slip and filled out application [form](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/forms/birth.pdf).  Only the following can request for a copy of the document: person himself/herself, any authorized personnel, spouse, parents, direct descendants, guardian, or institution legally in charge (in case of minor). 

Depending on the branch and age of the requestor, processing time can range from 2-10 working days (ie. if the birth certificate is 1940 or older it will take longer because the documents are not yet digitally saved). 

**Note:** Check the PSA website for the latest application form. Due to the covid-19 protocols, there could be additional health forms that need to be submitted.

**[Link](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)**

**Appointment:** [**https://psa.gov.ph/**](https://psa.gov.ph/)

**Cost: Php 155.00** per copy 

2. Online Request – The document can be requested online. Only the following can request for a copy of the document: person himself/herself, any authorized personnel, spouse, parents, direct descendants, guardian, or institution legally in charge (in case of minor). Processing can be paid via credit card online or through an ATM. Mobile wallet like GCash, ECPay, PayMaya, 7-Eleven shops, Palawan Express, and Dragonpay are also accepted as payment methods. 

The document will be delivered anywhere in the Philippines within 3 to 4 working days after payment confirmation inside Metro Manila and 3 to 8 working days outside Metro Manila.

**Link:** [PSAHelpline](https://psahelpline.ph/) or [PSA serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)

**Cost: Php 365.00 per copy** 

3. Claiming – If the requestor is not able to receive the document, an authorize representative can claim from the branch /accredited center or receive the document from the courier (for online deliver). Requirements to bring: 

- Signed authorization letter / Special power of attorney (SPA), stating the facts of the requested documents, number of copies and purpose
- Valid government ID (with photo and signature) or passport of the requestor
- Valid government ID (with photo and signature) or passport of the authorized representative

## Personal status certificate from The Philippines

Name in the Philippines : CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage)

**How to obtain?**

CENOMAR can be obtained from the Philippine Statistics office (PSA) in-person or online. Refer to the procedure for requesting a birth certificate.

**Cost: Php 430.00**

[**Link**](https://psa.gov.ph/civilregistration/requesting-civil-registry-document/cenomar)

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from The Philippines

Name in the Philippines: NBI Clearance (National Bureau 

**How to obtain?**

Register to the NBI Clearance website and create an account. Schedule an appointment for the biometrics and pay the processing fee to confirm your slot. On the day of your appointment, bring the appointment slip, application form and 2 valid IDs (refer to the website for the list of valid IDs). 

If you are located outside the Philippines, contact the Philippine embassy in your country of residence to start the process. Request an appointment from the embassy to obtain the biometrics and authorization letter for the NBI clearance. Once you have these documents, send them to the authorized representative in the Philippines. Only the authorized personnel can process the clearance on behalf of the requestor. 

**Cost: Php 155.00** (in the Philippines) / Additional fees may apply when processing from outside the Philippines

**[Link](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph/)**

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in The Philippines

**How to obtain?**

Documents can be apostilled in in all Authenticating Units of the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA): DFA-Aseana; DFA Consular Office (CO) Northeast (Ali Mall, Cubao); CO East (SM Megamall); CO West (SM Manila); CO South (Metro Gaisano, Alabang); CO San Fernando, Pampanga; CO Davao (SM Davao); CO Iloilo; and CO Cebu (Pacific Mall-Metro Mandaue).

**Cost:** **Php 100.00** for regular processing (released after three working days);

**Php 200.00** for expedited processing (to be released the following working day)

**[Link](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=97#:~:text=An%20Apostille%20is%20a%20certificate,party%20to%20the%20Apostille%20Convention)**

**Additional tip** – Couriers services between Israel and the Philippines: **[Fedex](https://www.fedex.com/en-il/customer-support.html), [USPS](https://www.usps.com/), [DHL](https://www.dhl.com/il-en/home.html)**
