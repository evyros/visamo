# Where these files come from

The same files as in evals/checks/foreignCivilStatus/files (see its
SOURCES.md): real-affidavit.pdf and real-certificate.pdf are real, shared by the app's
owner (an affidavit of civil status and no children, signed before a notary
in Israel, and the civil-status certificate it was filed with), and
affidavit-not-notarized.pdf is the affidavit's pages without the notary's
page and stamp. affidavit-emily.pdf, an affidavit of no children, is made up
for the evals, before a made-up notary in Israel.
