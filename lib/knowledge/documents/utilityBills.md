# Bills for your home (חשבונות של הדירה)

Bills for your shared house from the last 12 months, in the landlord's name or yours, for example: electricity bill, water bill, arnona bill, phone bill, etc.

The bills of the home you live in together, or lived in together, in Israel
or abroad: for example electricity, water, arnona (municipal tax) and phone.
The AS/6 checklist asks for them in the landlord's name or the Israeli
partner's, for a period you state, from one date to another. In practice,
bills in either of your names are accepted too. They're part of
the proof that your center of life is in that home. If you've never lived
together, the Israeli partner brings the bills of their own home instead
(the bills for the Israeli partner's home).

## How to get it

From each provider of the home: the electricity company, the water
corporation or the municipality, the municipality for arnona, the phone
company, and any other provider of the home (gas, internet, TV). Most let
you download past bills from your personal area on their website or app.

## What it should include

- **At least 3 types of household bills:** electricity, water, arnona,
  phone, gas, internet, TV or vaad bayit (building committee). Any bill of
  the home counts.
- **The last 12 months,** the period your center of life is proven for.
- **In the landlord's name, or in either or both of your names.**
- **The home's address** on each bill, the same as on your lease.
- **If you live, or lived, together abroad:** the bills of the home you
  share, or shared, there, in both your names (recommended), or either of
  yours. They need no apostille. If they aren't in Hebrew, Arabic or
  English, translate them yourselves: no notary is needed, unless the office
  asks (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## Questions

**The bills are in the landlord's name. Is that a problem?**
No. Bills in the landlord's name or yours are accepted.

**Which bills do we need?**
At least 3 types of household bills, for the last 12 months. Any bill of
the home counts: electricity, water, arnona, phone, gas, internet, TV or
vaad bayit. Bring more if your home has them.
