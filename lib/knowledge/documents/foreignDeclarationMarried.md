# Foreign partner's declaration (form AS/6) (הצהרת בן הזוג המוזמן (טופס אש/6))

Page 5 of the form below: the foreign partner's declaration (הצהרת בן הזוג המוזמן).

The foreign partner's own declaration (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ז), one of the parts of
form AS/6 (אש/6). If the foreign partner is abroad and the Israeli partner
files from Israel, the form is filed without it, and the foreign partner
signs it once they arrive in Israel with the entry permit. If you both live
abroad, or the foreign partner is already in Israel, it's filed with the
rest of the form.

## How to get it

It's page 5 of form AS/6 on the Population and Immigration Authority's site
(see [sources.md](../sources.md)), titled **הצהרת בן הזוג המוזמן**
("declaration of the invited partner"). The form also comes in another
format, with its pages in a different order: in a copy from elsewhere, find
the page by its title. It's in Hebrew, so it needs no translation.

1. Fill in the foreign partner's first name, last name and ID number.
2. Tick (☒) the box next to each statement they declare, and explain any
   they can't, in the lines under the list.
3. Write the place and the date, and sign it **in front of a lawyer (עו"ד)
   or a Misrad Hapnim (משרד הפנים) registrar (רשם)**, who confirms the
   declaration at the bottom of the page. Don't sign it before.

## What it should include

- **The foreign partner's details:** first name, last name and ID number (מ"ז).
- **A tick next to each statement**. By ticking, the foreign partner declares
  that:
  - they were told that getting Israeli citizenship may cancel their other
    citizenship, and were advised to check this with their country's
    authorities before deciding;
  - they intend to settle in Israel;
  - they aren't addicted to hard drugs or alcohol, and don't have a disease
    that could endanger public health in Israel;
  - they haven't committed a criminal offense (other than traffic offenses),
    have no pending case or arrest warrant, and aren't wanted by the police
    or security authorities of any country;
  - they haven't acted against the Jewish people or the security of the
    State of Israel, or been a member or a supporter of a body acting against
    it;
  - they've never had a deportation or removal order from Israel;
  - the marriage is real, and wasn't made to get status in Israel;
  - they're married only to the Israeli partner, who is married only to them;
  - they know the Population and Immigration Authority doesn't have to give
    status in Israel to children from a previous relationship.
- **An explanation for any statement they can't declare**, in the lines
  under the list.
- **The place, the date and the foreign partner's signature.**
- **The lawyer's or registrar's confirmation** (אישור קבלת ההצהרה): their
  name and license number, the foreign partner's name and the ID it was
  checked against, and their stamp and signature.

## Questions

**My partner is still abroad. Do they sign their declaration there?**
No. They sign it once they're in Israel, in front of an Israeli lawyer or a
Misrad Hapnim registrar. Until then, the Israeli partner files the form
without it.

**Do we file the whole form again?**
No. The foreign partner fills in and signs only their declaration.
