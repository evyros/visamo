# Letter about your relationship (מכתב על הקשר ביניכם)

A letter signed by both of you: how and when you met, your relationship and your life together.

A shared explanation letter, written and signed by both of you, about your
relationship (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ו): how you met, how long you've been together,
and your life together.

## How to get it

You write it yourselves, together, preferably in Hebrew. Both of you sign
it.

## What it should include

- **Your relationship:** how you met, how long you've been together, and
  how it developed.
- **Anecdotes** about your relationship, and **major life events** you've
  shared.
- **Your life together** today.
- **Each of your life histories,** where it's relevant - It's not required.
- **Usually 3–4 paragraphs long.**
- **Both your signatures.**

## Questions

**Does the letter have to be in Hebrew?**
Preferably, yes.

**Do we write a new letter when we renew the visa?**
Yes. When you renew, the letter explains what happened since you received
the visa, or since your last renewal: the major life events you've shared.
