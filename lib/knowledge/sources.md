# Sources

## Where this knowledge comes from

The knowledge here is drawn from five places:

- **Firsthand experience.** Evyatar (אביתר), Visamo's founder, went through the
  whole process with his American wife.
- **Interviews with couples.** Couples who finished the process were
  interviewed about every step: what they were asked for, where they got
  stuck, and how they got past it.
- **Immigration lawyers.** Leading Israeli immigration lawyers were
  consulted to check the complicated cases and the edge cases, and to
  settle unclear situations where more than one path could be right.
- **Misrad Hapnim's procedures.** The Population and Immigration
  Authority's published procedures and forms (below), and how they've
  changed over the years.
- **What's shared online.** Lawyers' articles, Facebook groups, Reddit and
  other forums, to learn how things work in practice and what couples run
  into. Nothing from these was added before it was carefully checked.

Brought together, these make Visamo the most complete and reliable
knowledge base about the Israeli partner visa, more than any other source.

## Procedures of the Population and Immigration Authority

- **5.2.0008**: granting status to a foreigner married to an Israeli citizen.
  Edition 16, updated 20 July 2026.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/status_foreigner_married_to_israeli_procedure/he/5.2.0008.pdf
  - §ד.2: the documents to open a file.
  - §ד.4: applicants from the former USSR are referred to Nativ.
  - §ג.7–ג.8: the B/1 while the file is checked isn't counted in the
    gradual process, which starts with the A/5.
  - §ה.1(6): couples who both lived abroad file online within 30 days of the
    foreign partner's entry.
  - §ה.2(1): who is rejected at the start.
  - §ה.2(7): the center-of-life check.
  - §ה.2(9): children from a previous relationship, and the other parent.
  - §ג.9 and §ו.2: a deposit, at any stage or as a condition for starting
    the gradual process.
  - §ג.22: an internal appeal, within 21 days of the decision.
  - Police certificates from other countries: from each country lived in for
    at least 6 months in a row (in both procedures since January 2024).
- **5.2.0009**: common-law couples, including same-sex couples.
  Edition 17, updated 16 July 2026.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/israelis_couples_status_procedure/he/5.2.0009..pdf
  - §ה.1.ה: couples who both lived abroad file online within 30 days of the
    foreign partner's entry.
  - Clause 10 (page 16): the interview is held, whenever possible, when the
    couple shows the original documents.
  - §ג.6 and §ו.2: a deposit, at any stage or as a condition for starting
    the gradual process.
  - §ג.18: an internal appeal, within 21 days of the decision.
- **5.1.0004**: the Authority's general guarantees procedure: who may ask for
  how much, how a deposit is paid, returned or forfeited. Updated 15 January
  2026.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/procedure_guarantees_2012/he/5.1.0004.pdf
- **5.4.0001**: visit permits for tourists, including visits approved in
  advance.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/procedure_for_handling_tourist_visa_application_b2/he/5.4.0001.pdf
- **1.3.0001**: foreign documents (certification and translation).
- **5.1.0013**: how the Authority conducts interviews.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/making_interviews_procedure/he/5.1.0013.pdf
- **5.1.0003**: exemption from the fees, in extreme cases.
  https://www.gov.il/he/departments/policies/policy_exemption_from_pay_for_residency_permit
- **2.11.0001**: registering changes in civil status, including marriages.

## Forms

- **AS/6** (אש/6): the status application for a married couple, with its
  checklist of attachments.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS6.pdf
- **AS/1** (אש/1): the entry permit application, for a foreign partner outside
  Israel.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS1.pdf
- **AS/3** (אש/3): the application to extend a permit of residence or change
  the visa category, for a foreign partner already in Israel.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS3.pdf
- **MR/6** (מר/6): the notice of change in civil status, to register a
  marriage abroad in the population registry. You sign it when you register
  the marriage, before you apply. The online application asks for it: if
  the Israeli partner is already registered as married, upload their
  appendix (ספח) instead.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/mr_6.pdf
- **The common-law affidavit** (5.2.0009_a): the declaration both partners
  sign with an application under procedure 5.2.0009.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/5.2.0009_a.pdf
