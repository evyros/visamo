# CV for the security screening (קורות חיים לאבחון)

For a foreign partner who's a Palestinian resident or a citizen of an Arab country: a full history of their employment and life, on a form in Hebrew and Arabic. Not a job CV.

The CV for the security screening (קורות חיים לאבחון), for applicants who
go through a security check (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ט). It's required only of a
foreign partner who is a Palestinian resident or a citizen of an Arab
country (see [security-check.md](../security-check.md)).
It isn't a job CV: it's a full history of the foreign partner's employment
and life, for security clearance.

## What it should include

- **The form itself,** bilingual in Hebrew and Arabic, filled in.
- **A full history** of the foreign partner's employment and life.
