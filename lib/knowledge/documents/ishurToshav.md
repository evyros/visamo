# Municipality confirmation of your center of life (אישור עירייה/מועצה על מרכז חיים ביישוב)

Also called Ishur Toshav (אישור תושב): your municipality or local council's official confirmation that your center of life is in their locality.

The municipality or local council's confirmation that your center of life
is in their locality.

- **Married couples:** always bring it when at least one of you lives in
  Israel. It's on the AS/6 checklist, the married couples' application.
- **Common-law couples:** it isn't part of your application (procedure
  5.2.0009), and in practice you're rarely asked for it. Bring it only if
  the clerk asks for it.

The municipality issues it only to someone registered with it as the payer
of arnona (municipal tax) for the home. Usually the arnona department issues
it.

## How to get it

- **Online:** some municipalities issue it on their website, after you log
  in to your account there.
- **In person:** many don't. It's very common that when you ask for it, the
  municipality says they don't know the document, or can't issue it. Then
  book an appointment, go in person, and say you need it for Misrad Hapnim (משרד הפנים)
  (the Population and Immigration Authority): they'll understand what you
  mean.
- **A small local council or a kibbutz** can write a confirmation that
  you're registered in the kibbutz's books (אישור על רישום בספרי הקיבוץ).

It's an Israeli document, in Hebrew: no certification or translation.

## If you don't have it

If neither of you is registered for arnona (the landlord pays it, say), the
municipality can't issue it. Bring instead a short letter explaining why, and
a confirmation from the landlord that the arnona for the home is in their
name.

## Questions

**Is it really required? Can we file without it?**
For married couples, yes: it's part of the AS/6 application when at least
one of you lives in Israel. Common-law couples rarely need it: only if the
clerk asks for it. If you can't get it because neither of you is registered
for arnona, see "If you don't have it" above.

**Can I just bring an arnona (ארנונה) bill instead?**
No, an arnona bill doesn't replace this document. Arnona shows you pay for
a property, not necessarily that you live there. You must get this document
from the municipality to support your file's claim that you live there.

**The municipality says they don't issue this document. What do we do?**
It's very common. Book an appointment, go in person, and say you need it
for Misrad Hapnim: they'll understand what you mean. If you aren't registered
for arnona, they can't issue it: see "If you don't have it" above.
