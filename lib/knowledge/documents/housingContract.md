# Lease or purchase contract (חוזה שכירות או רכישה)

The lease or purchase contract of your home in the Israeli partner's name: the original, a copy and the lease appendix.

The lease or purchase contract of your home, in the Israeli partner's name:
the original, a copy, and the lease appendix.

It's asked for when you live together, or have lived together, and whenever
the Israeli partner lives in Israel, even if you've never lived together:
then it's the lease or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home. If
you've never lived together and the Israeli partner lives abroad, see what
to bring instead in [documents.md](../documents.md).

**If you lived together abroad:** bring the lease or purchase contract of
the home you shared there. A contract that isn't in Hebrew or Arabic may
need a translation (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).
