# Documents for the first application

The documents to open your file with Misrad Hapnim. Sources: procedure
5.2.0008 §ד.2 (married couples), the checklist attached to form AS/6, and
how offices work in practice. Each document is described below, by group.

## Good to know first

- **Center of life:** proof that you live together in Israel has to cover
  **at least the last 12 months**.
- **Copies:** where a number of copies is given (the AS/6 checklist writes
  "2X"), bring that many.
- **Translation:** see [certification.md](certification.md). By law, only
  Hebrew and Arabic need no translation.
- **The fee** is paid with the application; it isn't a document.
- The clerk can ask for any other document the circumstances call for (§ד.3).
