# תצהיר ידועים בציבור / Affidavit for “Yeduim Batzibur” (Common-Law Partners)

The *Affidavit for “Yeduim Batzibur” (Common-Law Partners)*, is an affidavit that is sometimes required by PIBA (the Population and Immigration Authority) or other entities in Israel. **It is used as a way to confirm the official relationship status of non-married couples**.

An affidavit is a legal document, and **giving a false affidavit is a punishable criminal offense.** Before proceeding, learn more information about [affidavits in Israel](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/).

The Israeli Association for International Couples has created bilingual, pre-structed, lawyer-reviewed affidavit formats, for the personal use of international couples in Israel.
