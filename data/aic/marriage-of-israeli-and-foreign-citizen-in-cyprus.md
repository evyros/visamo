# Marriage of Israeli and Foreign Citizen in Cyprus

Below you can find a description of the procedure for a civil wedding in Cyprus, between an Israeli citizen and a Slovenian citizen. As none of the parties is local to Cyprus, it can be assumed that this procedure is relevant for other nationalities, too.

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

We had the most simple wedding with only the two of us. Everything was easy to organize. The municipality in Larnaca responded very fast and clearly on every email. The ceremony is in English and in case you don’t have witnesses they can arrange it for you.

## Documents needed from the Israeli side

- Recent Single Certificate (from last 3 months)

Israel doesn’t explicitly issue a “single status certificate” or a “certificate of non-impediment” (“nulla osta”) which is required by most EU countries in order to marry. However, Cyprus accepts the Israeli “equivalent” which is the “Population Registry Extract” (תמצית רישום). [Click here to find out how to issue a Tamtzit Rishum](https://aic.org.il/apply-and-issue-an-israeli-registry-extract-or-tamtzit-rishum-%d7%aa%d7%9e%d7%a6%d7%99%d7%aa-%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%a9%d7%95%d7%9d/)!

- Passport with validity of at least 6 months when entering Cyprus.

- Additional certificates:

Some municipalities in Cyprus may ask you to show a birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, a divorce certificate (when applicable) etc.

All the documents (other than the passport) have to be translated to English in a notarized translation (unless the originals are in English) and authenticated with an Apostille.

## Documents needed from the other side

- Recent Single Certificate

At the registry office/administrative unit of your home town without previous appointment. Go personally or authorise someone. You get the certificate on the spot.

- Birth Certificate

             Same as Single Certificate.

- Divorce Certificate in case of a Divorce
- Passport

All the documents have to be translated in English by an official translator and with an Apostille.

At the district court. Bring all the documents with you and hand them in. It usually takes three days for them to attach the apostille.

Cost**:** 2,46 EUR per document

With help of the link find the authorised translator that is the closest to you. Cost: varies Link: [https://spvt.mp.gov.si/tolmaci.html](https://spvt.mp.gov.si/tolmaci.html)

## Procedure (what does one have to do before, during, and after the marriage)

1. By email contact Civil Marriage office in Larnaca ([civilmarriage@larnaka.com](mailto:civilmarriage@larnaka.com)), to set the date and time of the wedding.
2. When you prepare all the documents that are translated and with apostille send them by email to them so they can check that everything is ok and there will be no surprises on the day of the wedding. 
3. On the day of the wedding bring the original documents with you and the money for all the fees.

*The fees are* :      

·       **281.90 EUR** for the ceremony which includes one Marriage Certificate.

·       **13.65 EUR** for each extra marriage certificate you may need.

·       **20.00 EUR** for one Apostille (Ministry of Justice) and additional **5.00 EUR** for each extra certificate you may need.

The payment should be made in cash in **EUR** the day of the wedding.

### Registration of the marriage in your home counties

In Slovenia you do it at administrative unit of your home town. If you are changing the surname you are filling in the form on the spot and then accordingly change all the documents. It is required that you do this within 30 days after the marriage but personally they were really nice and let me do this later because of the logistic reasons and me having temporary residency outside Slovenia. You can also do all of this in the Slovenian Embassy if you are living abroad, but it takes long time and the fees are much higher.

In Israel you should print and complete the Change of marital status form ([https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/change\_personal\_status/he/mr6.pdf](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/change_personal_status/he/mr6.pdf))  and submit it in person at your local Population and Immigration Authority office. You should bring marriage certificate translated with apostille, a recent Single status certificate of a foreigner spouse, also translated with apostille, your own ID and a photocopy of spouses passport.
