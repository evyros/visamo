# Bills for your home (חשבונות של הדירה)

Electricity, water, arnona and phone bills for the last 12 months, in the renter's name or the Israeli partner's.

Bills (electricity, water, arnona, phone), **in the renter's name or the
Israeli partner's**.

- **If you don't live together:** the bills of the Israeli partner's home,
  in the Israeli partner's name.

**If you lived together abroad:** bring the bills of the home you shared
there. Bills that aren't in Hebrew or Arabic may need a translation (see
[certification.md](../certification.md)).
