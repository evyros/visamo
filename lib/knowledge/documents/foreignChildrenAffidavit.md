# Affidavit about children (תצהיר על ילדים)

The foreign partner's affidavit, signed in front of a lawyer, about their children from before the marriage or from a previous marriage, and who has guardianship of them. If they have no children, it says so.

An affidavit (תצהיר) of the foreign partner about their children from before
the marriage (from the time they were single) or from a previous marriage,
and about guardianship of those children. Its full name: "תצהיר לגבי ילדים
מרווקות או מנישואין קודמים, ואפוטרופסות על הילדים". Every foreign partner brings one,
whether or not they have children: if they have none, the affidavit says
so.

## How to get it

A lawyer writes it, or you write it and the foreign partner signs it **in
front of a lawyer (עו"ד)**, who confirms it. Signed abroad, it's signed in
front of a notary there, and needs that country's certification (see
[certification.md](../certification.md)).

## What it should include

- **The foreign partner's details:** their full name and passport number.
- **Their children** from before the marriage or from a previous marriage:
  each child's name and date of birth, and who has guardianship of them.
- **If they have no children:** a statement saying so.
- **The signature,** and the lawyer's confirmation, with the lawyer's
  stamp.
- **A certified translation**, unless it's in Hebrew or Arabic. In practice,
  English is usually accepted without one.

## Questions

**My partner has no children. Do we still need it?**
Yes. Every foreign partner brings it: without children, it states that they
have none.

**My partner's children aren't moving to Israel. Do they go in the affidavit?**
Yes. It's about all their children from before the marriage or a previous
marriage, and who has guardianship of them. Children who move with them also
need their own documents (see [children.md](../children.md)).
