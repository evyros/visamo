# Partner Visa Application Or Renewal: List of Required Documents

Below you’ll find a **list of the documents** you will need to upload or present when applying for the Israeli partner visa for the first time or when applying for it’s renewal or upgrade. This list is relevant for both married and unmarried couples.

Find additional information in our [Step-by-Step Guide to Your First Visa](https://aic.org.il/partner-visa-in-israel-step-by-step-guide-to-your-first-visa/), and our of [Partner Visa Renewal or Upgrade Procedures](https://aic.org.il/partner-visa-b1-or-a5-renewal-procedures/).

Pay attention – document requirements for visa renewals vary between the different PIBA branches. The most accurate information about documentation can only be given to you by your PIBA office.

## Authenticated Certificates for First Application

The authenticated documents needed to start the partnership visa process in Israel are:

- **Birth Certificate**
- **Name Change Certificate** (when applicable)
- **Personal Status Certificate**
- **Certificate of Non-Criminal Record**
- **Marriage Certificate** (for married couples)

These documents only have to be submitted when applying for a partner visa for the first time, or when there is a change to your status or name.

Read the full explanation and information about [official certificates from the country of origin of the foreign partner](https://aic.org.il/official-documentation-for-the-partner-visa/).

## Forms and IDs for First Application

1. Form [AS/1](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS1.pdf) (אש/1) – required only when the foreign partner is outside of Israel.
2. Form [AS/3](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS3.pdf) (אש/3) – required only when the foreign partner is already in Israel.
3. Form [AS/6](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS6.pdf) (אש/6) and form [MR/6](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/mr_6.pdf) (מר/6) – required only for married couples. Form MR/6 is not required if the Israeli already updated their status to “married” in their ID.
4. Israeli ID (Israeli side)
5. Passport (foreign side) with a validity of **at least 2 years**.
6. 2 passport photos of each person (Check this [guide](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/general/biometric_photo_guide) about the photo requirements).

## Forms and IDs for Renewal/Upgrade Application

1. Form [AS/3](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/AS3.pdf) (אש/3)
2. Israeli ID (Israeli side)
3. Passport (foreign side) with a validity of **at least 6 additional months after the expiry of the requested visa** – if your passport does not meet this requirement, you might get a shorter visa until you renew your passport.
4. 2 passport photos of each person (Check this [guide](https://www.gov.il/en/departments/general/biometric_photo_guide) about the photo requirements).

## “Center of Life” Documents and Relationship Proofs – For All Applications

1. **Shared explanation letter** – a letter written and signed by both partners, preferably in Hebrew, explaining your relationship, how you met, how long you’ve been together, any anecdotes about your relationship, etc. (When renewing the visa, the letter should include an explanation about what happened since you received the visa/your last renewal, major shared life events, etc.).  

2. **Shared photos** – photos of both partners together, preferably in different locations, and from different times. It is highly recommended to include some photos with more people – family and friends. When using the online form, we suggest uploading at least 10 photos. It is advisable to prepare more photos, in a photo grid of 9/16 photos per page and attach more.  

3. **Messaging/call history** – up to 10 pages of your conversation history over email, Whatsapp, social media, or any other messaging application, and/or transcription of calls between each other.  

4. **Bank statement** – a statement of a shared bank account or statements of two separate personal accounts. The required timeframe for the statement changes between PIBA branches, we recommend bringing a statement for the full 12 months. The bank statement should include the balance and all incoming/outgoing transactions, alongside the proof of account ownership.  

5. **Proof of getting services from governmental authorities** (אישור קבלת שירותים ממוסדות ממשלתיים) – This is relevant only to the Israeli side or to individuals who have been living in Israel – any proof you can show that you’ve been getting governmental services. It can be certificates/documents from the Tax Authority (רשות המיסים), the Ministry of Transportation (משרד הרישוי), National Insurance (ביטוח לאומי), etc. You can present, for example, your vehicle license, your driver’s license, the annual tax report, proof of unemployment benefits, etc.  

6. **Rent contract or proof of house ownership** (from Israel only) – if the foreign side is still abroad, only of the Israeli side. If you live with the parents – a signed letter from them, with their ID copies attached, stating that you live at their place (a signed affidavit from the parents or the landlord might be required instead). Married couples are required to submit an affidavit from their landlord/house owners as part of form AS6. This requirement is sometimes waived.  

7. **Utility bills of the last 12 months** (from Israel only) – if the foreign side is still abroad, only of the Israeli side. This includes electric bills, water, municipality (Arnona), gas bills (when applicable), etc. If the bills are not in your name – prepare a short written clarification that they are in the name of the landlord/parent.  

8. **Recommendation letters from Israeli family/friends** – At least 3 letters in Hebrew, signed, and with a copy of their Israeli ID attached. For more information and tips about recommendation letters – visit [“Partner visa Recommendation Letters”](https://aic.org.il/partner-visa-recommendation-letters-info-tips/).

## Additional Documents

PIBA may require the following documents during your partner visa application process or during any stage of the gradual process itself:

1. **Declaration of the truthfulness of the submitted material** (תצהיר על אמיתות החומר שהוגש…) –
    - [Form for non-married couples](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/5.2.0009_a.pdf)
    - [Form for married couples\*](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/visas_forms/he/5.2.0009_a1.pdf)
        - \*Married couples who have submitted Form AS6 are usually not required to submit this separately, as this declaration is already included in Form AS6.  

2. **Proof of employment** – Present an employment approval from an Israeli employer and 12 last paychecks (only for people employed in Israel), OR a signed approval by an accountant of your last year’s reported income (for independent workers).  

3. **Proof of income/financial means** – sometimes required at a later stage, either from the Israeli side or non-Israeli side, especially in cases of unemployment. It is recommended to include an explanation letter about your financial situation and any related proof (getting help from family, scholarships, internships, etc).  

4. **Confirmation of registration to school, semester certificates, school/kindergarten secretary approval** – applicable when there are children above the age of 6 living in Israel.  

5. **Proof of active studies** – applicable for academic students.  

6. **Driver’s license** – if you have one.  

7. **Vehicle license** – if you own a vehicle.  

8. **Proof of health insurance** – for Israelis, it’s an approval that you are registered with National Insurance and that you are a member of an HMO (אישור חברות בקופת חולים, להורדה מהאיזור האישי באתר ביטוח לאומי). For foreigners – approval that you have health insurance coverage in Israel.  

9. [**CV for diagnosis (קורות חיים לאבחון)**](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/servicequestionnaire/requesting-status-for-foreign-life-partner/he/%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%20%D7%97%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D%20%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9F.pdf) – Only required of non-Israeli partners who are either Palestinian residents or a citizens of an Arab country. The form is bilingual in Hebrew and Arabic.  

10. **Any additional proof of shared life abroad or in Israel** (when applicable).

Some of these additional, non-mandatory documents have dedicated fields in the online form. If you have these documents, it is recommended to submit them in the online form in the dedicated field.

## Affidavits

In some cases (especially in cases where you can’t get official documents or governmental proof of something), PIBA will require a validated affidavit. An affidavit is a written statement, voluntarily made by an affiant*,* under an oath or affirmation which is administered by a person who is authorized to do so by law. In Israel, all licensed lawyers are allowed to validate your affidavit, unless you were specifically asked for a notarial affidavit. In this case, the affidavit will have to be signed in front of a notary.

You can find out more about the affidavits in Israel, and how to make them, here: [“Affidavits in Israel”](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/).

The Israeli Association for International Couples has created pre-structured affidavits to cover the majority of cases in partner visa applications. Please [contact us](https://aic.org.il/help-and-services/) for more information.

**The most commonly required affidavits are:**

1. תצהיר לגבי ילדים מרווקות או מנישואין קודמים, ואפוטרופסות על הילדים / An affidavit about children from previous singleness period or previous marriage, and guardianship of children.
2. תצהיר לגבי מצב אישי עדכני או קודם / An affidavit about current or previous personal status.
3. תצהיר רווקות / Single status affidavit.
