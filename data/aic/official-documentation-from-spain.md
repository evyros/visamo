# Official Documentation from Spain

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

\*This article is covering the procedure to obtain documentation in the autonomous community of **País Vasco**. Please take into account that the described procedure may vary in other parts of Spain.

## Birth Certificate from Spain

Name in Spain: Certificado de Nacimiento.

**How to obtain?**

Go to the “registro civil” (civil registry). I personally showed up there in person but one can also call their local civil registry office to make an appointment. There’s the option to request it and send it to any address nationally or internationally. In fact, at another occasion I got it sent to the embassy and picked it up there.

This document has to be with you before you pick up the criminal record (see below the explanation), since it is there where you collect the apostilles for the 3 documents.

**Cost:** free

**[Link](https://www.euskadi.eus/web01-citaregc/es/aa14aUIWar/justizia/aa14aRegistroCivilInit.jsp?lang=es)** (Basque Country)

## Personal status certificate from Spain

Name in Spain: Certificado de **fe de vida** y estado.

**How to obtain?**

In Bilbao, Basque Country, you make the appointment online, select a date and go pick it up in Barroeta Aldamar, 10 street. You need to have this document before the appointment of the Criminal Record, since that is the place where you get the apostilles.

**Cost:** Free.

[**Link**](https://www.justizia.eus/qmaticwebbooking/#/preselect/servicegroups/Registro?lang=es_es) (Basque Country)

**Email** (Basque country): [secretario\_general.bizkaia@seap.minhap.es](mailto:secretario_general.bizkaia@seap.minhap.es)

**Telephone** (Basque country): 944509485

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Spain

Name in Spain: Antecedentes penales.

**How to obtain?**

Make an appointment online in the sub delegation of the government in Elcano Kalea, 10, 48009 Bilbo, Bizkaia. In my case, I picked it up in Bilbao in the sub delegation of the government.

![](https://spcdn.shortpixel.ai/spio2/ret_img+q_cdnize+to_webp+s_dwebp/aic.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/image.jpg)

Website of the appointment management system

For the criminal record, you need the following documents in the day of the appointment:

- Form 790 (download it from the site) completed and signed, where it is stated that you have paid a fee of **3.86** EUR for each certificate you request, at any collaborating bank (Santander, Bankinter…). You need to go before you go pick up the documents and the apostilles to the bank to pay this fee.
- Original or certified photocopy of the valid identity document with which you have obtained the appointment (DNI, NIE, Passport, Spanish driving license or community identity document).

**Cost:** The document is free but you need to fill in the “790 form” which costs **3,86 EUR**.

select office by province and the document Antecedentes Penales.

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Spain

**How to obtain?**

You get the apostilles on the spot in the office where you pick up the criminal record. You just need to remind them you need the apostille and make sure you have the three documents by the time you go there to have it stamped.

If you want JUST the apostille because, for example, you forgot to ask for them when you picked up the documents, you can request it on the same website as the above screenshot, in the “Servicio” field, and it is free and a maximum of 3 documents can be apostilled at a time.

**Cost: Free**
