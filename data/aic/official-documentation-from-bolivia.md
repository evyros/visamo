# Official Documentation from Bolivia

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Bolivia

Name in Bolivia: Certificado de Nacimiento

**How to obtain?**

You must physically go to the office of Registro Civil de Bolivia or the office Sereci close to your birth place. The issuing process takes only a few minutes.

If you can’t do it yourself, these are the people that can apply on your behalf: someone with a notary power, someone with legal custody, your wife/husband, children, siblings, parents or tutors (all the above should be with minimum age of 18 years old). The requirements when soliciting this document is to have a valid ID.

**Cost:** At Registro Civil > **50 Bs. (7.3$)**

At Sereci > **30 Bs. (4.4$)** – there might be longer queuing times here.

**[Link](https://www.municipio.com.bo/certificado-de-nacimiento.html)**

You can check the [Sereci website](http://foroconsular.oep.org.bo/sereci/) for offices address, telephone number and opening hours.

## Name change certificate from Bolivia

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in Bolivia: –

**How to obtain?**

There isn’t such a certificate in Bolivia. When you change your name they change it also in your birth certificate. For more details, inquire at [Sereci](http://foroconsular.oep.org.bo/sereci/).

## Personal status certificate from Bolivia

Name in Bolivia: Certificado de Estado Civil or Certificado de Solteria.

**How to obtain?**

You must go physically to the office of Registro Civil de Bolivia or the office Sereci, and present the following documents (original and copy of each):

- Be part of el Padron Electoral Biometrico.
- Original and valid ID of the solicitant or proxy.
- Birth Certificate.
- Letter of request granted by the office.
- In case of carrying out the procedure for a third party, a specific power of attorney must be presented that grants the powers to obtain the certificate.
- Proof of payment must be presented to authorized banks.

The validity of the document is 3 months.

**Cost:** **23 Bs. (3.4$)**

**[Link](https://www.municipio.com.bo/certificado-solteria.html)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Bolivia

Name in Bolivia: Certificado de Antecedentes Penales

**How to obtain?**

The administrative procedure to acquire the Criminal Record Certificate is personal and must be carried out in the Departmental Courts of Justice – Palacio de Justicia, at the customer service windows.

If the citizen interested in obtaining the document cannot personally go to the court offices, a third person may do so and request the certificate on their behalf by means of a notarized power of attorney.

The only requirements to carry out the procedure are the original ID or passport and a photocopy of the person interested in obtaining the document, and the payment for the issuance of the Criminal Record Certificate. Naturally, if a third person performs the procedure, they will also require the power of attorney of the citizen they represent.

**Cost:** **50 Bs. (7.3$)**

**[Link](https://www.municipio.com.bo/certificado-de-registro-judicial-de-antecedentes-penales.html)**

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Bolivia

**How to obtain?**

There are two different places for the apostille request, which one should be present in:

- **Sereci** – in which one can request apostille for Birth certificate, civil status certificate and marriage certificate.
- **Palacio de Justicia** – in which one can request apostille for criminal record certificate.

The steps for the application:

1. Request an appointment online for the [Sereci](https://programarcita.sereci-santacruz.com/requisitos/1) office or the [Palacio de Justicia office](http://apostilla.rree.gob.bo/#/solicitud).
2. Go to your appointment at the designated time.
3. Request a ticket to go to a customer service window.
4. Request the apostille for your certificate.
5. At the customer service window, they will tell you that you will receive an email with an account so you can pay to the bank.
6. Go to the bank to pay the apostille.
7. Upload a photo of the bank payment to the system.
8. The apostille will arrive to your email between 24 to 48 hours.

**Cost:**

For Birth Certificate – **115 Bs. total** (birth certificate+apostille). If your original birth certificate cost 50 Bs., then the apostille will cost 115-50=**65 Bs (9.5$)**.

For Personal Status Certificate – **105 Bs**. total (personal status certificate+apostille). If your original birth certificate cost 23 Bs., then the apostille will cost 105-23=**82 Bs (11.9$)**.

For Certificate of Criminal Record – **70 Bs. (10.2$)**.

[**Link**1](https://programarcita.sereci-santacruz.com/requisitos/1), **[Link2](http://apostilla.rree.gob.bo/#/solicitud)**

#### **Filling out the online forms to obtain apostille**

1\. For Birth Certificate and Personal Status Certificate:

Enter to the website link – https://programarcita.sereci-santacruz.com/requisitos/1, and fill the form. Through this form you can schedule an appointment with a fixed day and time for you to appear at the offices of SERECÍ.

Requirements:

- ID of the Holder and/or Applicant (original and photocopy).
- Be registered.
- In case of being a Legal Representative, present a Specific Power of Attorney to appear before the SERECÍ (in original and photocopy) expressing the authority to apostille the required document.
