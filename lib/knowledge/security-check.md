# The security check

- Applicants who need a security check fill in a **CV form for the
  screening** (קורות חיים לאבחון) (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ט). It's not a job CV: it's
  a history of your employment and life, for security clearance. The form is
  bilingual, in Hebrew and Arabic.
- The procedure refers to "risk countries" without listing them. In
  practice, it's required only of a foreign partner who is a **Palestinian
  resident or a citizen of an Arab country**.
