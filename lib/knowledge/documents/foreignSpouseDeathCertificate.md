# Death certificate of a previous spouse (תעודת פטירה של בן/בת זוג קודם/ת)

For each of the foreign partner's previous marriages that ended in the spouse's death.

For each of the foreign partner's previous marriages that ended in the
spouse's death: the previous spouse's death certificate (the AS/6
checklist). It's asked for as well as the proof of civil status: that one
shows their status, and the death certificate is the proof of how the
marriage ended.

## How to get it

It comes from the civil registry where the death was registered, in the
country where the spouse died. That isn't always the foreign partner's
country: a death registered elsewhere is proven by that country's
certificate, and certified there.

### Germany

A death certificate (Sterbeurkunde), from the civil registry office
(Standesamt) of the place of death.

### Russia

A death certificate (свидетельство о смерти), from the civil registry office
(ЗАГС). The apostille is put only on certificates issued in Russia after 26
December 1991: for an older one, ask for a new certificate (повторное
свидетельство).

### United Kingdom

A certified copy of the death entry, from the General Register Office (GRO)
or the register office where the death was registered. The apostille is put
only on that certified copy, never on a photocopy.

### United States

A certified copy of the death certificate, from the vital records office of
the state where the death was registered. Its apostille comes from that
state's Secretary of State.

## What it should include

- **One for each marriage** that ended in the spouse's death.
- **2 copies.**
- **The certification** of the country that issued it: an apostille, or
  consular legalization if that country isn't in the Apostille Convention
  (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).
- **A certified translation**, unless it's in Hebrew or Arabic. In practice,
  an English document is usually accepted without one, but the clerk can
  still ask you to translate it.

## Questions

**My partner's previous spouse died in another country. Where does the certificate come from?**
From the country where the death was registered, and it's certified there,
not in the country of your partner's nationality.

**Doesn't the proof of civil status already show my partner is widowed?**
At most it says "widowed": not which marriage ended, or when. The death
certificate is the proof of how that marriage ended.

**My partner was divorced from one spouse and widowed from another. What do we bring?**
Both: the divorce decree for the marriage that ended in divorce, and the
death certificate for the one that ended in the spouse's death.
