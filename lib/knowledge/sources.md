  Edition 16, updated 20 July 2026, 30 pages.
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/status_foreigner_married_to_israeli_procedure/he/5.2.0008.pdf
  - §ד.2: the documents to open a file (the main checklist).
  - §ד.4: former-USSR applicants are referred to Nativ.

    Education queries.
  - §ה.2(9): accompanying minors and the other parent.
  - Checked against the catalog in full for §ד.2 (catalog v2).
