# Contact Details and Information – All PIBA Offices with Visa Departments

All visa departments’ working hours are **Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 8:00 am to 12:00 pm** unless stated otherwise!

In this article, you will find the contact details of all PIBA (Misrad Hapnim) offices that have visa departments: their emails, addresses, and fax numbers.

All the ways to book an appointment with the visa department are listed here: [How to Book an Appointment to PIBA Visa Department](https://aic.org.il/how-to-book-an-appointment-to-piba-visa-department/).

**Application for the first partner visa** is done by a different online application form. Check out [our step-by-step guide](https://aic.org.il/partner-visa-in-israel-step-by-step-guide-to-your-first-visa/) on how to apply for your first partner visa.
