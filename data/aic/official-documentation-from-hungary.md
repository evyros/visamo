# Official Documentation from Hungary

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Hungary

Name in Hungary: Születési anyakönyvi kivonat

**How to obtain?**

In person at the Hungarian local civil registrar’s office / Anyakönyvvezető (in the city where you were born). OR online via Ügyfélkapu. OR via the Hungarian embassy.

**Cost: free**

[**Link**](https://kormanyablak.hu/hu/feladatkorok/22/ANYKV00010)

## Name change certificate from Hungary

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in Hungary: As far as I know we do not have a special document for this. In case you changed your name because of marriage, it will show your new married name in the Marriage Certificate.

**How to obtain?**

Same way as the Birth Certificate.

**Cost: free**

[**Link**](https://kormanyablak.hu/hu/feladatkorok/22/ANYKV00010)

## Personal status certificate from Hungary

Name in Hungary: Családi állapot igazolás

**How to obtain?**

You can get it from your Hungarian local district office /Kormányhivatal/, but it only shows the current personal Status. If you need previous status to be stated, you need to request the Clerk to do it for you manually, not from their system.

OR online via Ügyfélkapu, or via the Hungarian Embassy

C**ost: free**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Hungary

Name in Hungary: Hatósági erkölcsi bizonyítvány

**How to obtain?**

You can request it in any hungarian Post office and they will send you by post your Certificate to your hungarian address, OR online via Ügyfélkapu, OR via Hungarian embassy.

**Cost: Free up to 4 times per year.**

[**Link**](https://kormanyablak.hu/hu/feladatkorok/58/OKMIR00097)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Hungary

How to obtain?

In person, at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade. OR by post if you have an Hungarian address. OR via the Hungarian Embassy.

**Cost:** The consular fee of legalisation is **5,500 HUF** per signature

[**Link**](https://konzuliszolgalat.kormany.hu/information-on-the-legalisation-procedure-of-documents-to-be-used-abroad)
