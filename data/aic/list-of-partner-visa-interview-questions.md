# List of Partner Visa Interview Questions

In a shared effort with the community of international couples in Israel over on our [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/537939999618332), we’ve assembled a list of partner visa interview questions and arranged them by their frequency.

All of the questions listed here are **REAL QUESTIONS** that real couples were asked during their interviews at PIBA.

We remind you – there’s not need to panic over this list! You’re not going to be asked most of these questions. Not knowing or not remembering answers to some of these questions is totally legitimate. Please read our [elaborate post about the partner visa interview](https://aic.org.il/the-partner-visa-interview-everything-you-need-to-know/) for more information and tips!

The questions have been listed from Very Common to Uncommon based on the number of votes they got in our poll. Questions that will be asked only under certain specific circumstances were listed as Consequential. The poll was held during June 2022 and received 2,575 votes from at least 163 unique voters.

This information is made public and accessible for the first time ever thanks to the activity of The Israeli Association for International Couples. Do you find this article and list helpful? Please consider supporting the organization.
