# Official Documentation from Croatia

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Croatia

Name in Croatia : Izvadak iz maticne knjige rodenih, ili rodni list

**How to obtain?**

Go personally to any Maticni ured and fill out form requesting it. They should issue the document in 15 minutes and hand it to you.

**Cost: 20 HRK**, paid in drzavni biljezi

**Links:** **[Link 1](https://mpu.gov.hr/drzavne-matice-24504/24504), [Link 2](https://mpu.gov.hr/gradjani-21417/iz-djelokruga/drzavne-matice-24504/isprave-iz-drzavnih-matica-24506/24506)**

## Name change certificate from Croatia

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

## Personal status certificate from Croatia

Name in Croatia: Potvrda o slobodnom bracnom stanju

**How to obtain?**

Go personally to any Maticni ured and fill out form requesting it. They should issue the document in 15 minutes and hand it to you.

**Cost: 20 HRK**, paid in drzavni biljezi

**Links: [Link 1](https://mpu.gov.hr/drzavne-matice-24504/24504), [Link 2](https://mpu.gov.hr/gradjani-21417/iz-djelokruga/drzavne-matice-24504/isprave-iz-drzavnih-matica-24506/24506)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Croatia

Name in Croatia: Potvrda IZ KAZNENE EVIDENCIJE (not potvrda o nekaznjavanju)

How to obtain? Go personally to Ministarstvo pravosuda i uprave, fill out a form V.a (can be printed online beforehand from link below) and in around 15 minutes they will issue the certificate.

**Cost: 40 HRK**, payed in drzavni biljezi

**[Link](https://mpu.gov.hr/gdje-se-moze-ishoditi-uvjerenje-potvrda-o-nekaznjavanju-u-svrhu-zaposlenja/6684)**

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Croatia

**How to obtain?**

Go to Opcinski gradanski sud in person, depending on the place you got the documents from. Fill out a form. Depending on court, it can be 15 minutes or next working day, to pick up in person.

**Cost: 50 HRK per document**, if there are 2 or 3 documents apostilled at the same time, they will issue instructions on online paying – one document can be paid by biljeg 50 HRK (more than that cannot)

[**Link**](https://sudovi.hr/hr/ogszg/o-sudovima/ovjera-isprava-i-izdavanje-uvjerenja-za-inozemstvo-0)

**NOTE: It is not recommended to get digital version of these documents, through eGradani or similar, best to get original form with stamps signatures etc.**
