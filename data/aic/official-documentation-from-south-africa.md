# Official Documentation from South Africa

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from South Africa

Name in South Africa: Unabridged birth certificate (“UBC”)

**How to obtain?**

Apply at any branch of the Department of Home Affairs (“DHA”) in South Africa or through the South African Embassy in Ramat Gan. Agents in South Africa advertise that they can expedite applications for a birth certificate, however as of end 2021 the Government forbid any agents on applying on behalf of South Africans for personal documentation due to high volumes of fraud and scams.

Pay attention – Using agents who conduct business against the Government’s laws could land the agent and South African citizen in big trouble should they get caught.

The UBC includes the parents’ particulars of the applicant.  
  
To apply for an UBC the following documentation will be required by the DHA or Embassy (as per the DHA’s website):

- A duly completed Form BI-154, Application for Birth Certificate (this form must be completed in black ink).
- Pay the application fee (should it be required)

Processing time: As of 2022 the waiting period through DHA for a copy of an unabridged birth certificate has a turnaround of minimum 6 months up to 36 months (3 years).

**Cost**: **free** for the first copy, **R75** thereafter.

**[Link](http://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/civic-services/birth-certificates)**

## Personal status certificate from South Africa

Name in South Africa: Letter of Non Impediment (“LNI”)

**How to obtain?**

Before submitting an application, applicants should first verify that their marital status at the DHA is indeed correct. According to official DHA website, enquiries such as this one can be done by phone (**0800 60 11 90**) or by email (hacc@dha.gov.za). Unfortunately, contacting DHA by phone or email is unlikely to yield any results. In South Africa very little of the Government’s online systems work effectively and everything needs to be done in person at a DHA branch.

The applicant can apply for a LNI at any DHA branch in South Africa. Waiting period are currently at approximately 6 months and the LNI is only valid for 6 months from date of issue. 

**Cost**: **R50**

**[Link](http://www.dha.gov.za/)**

Because of the long waiting period for LNI, you could try to make an notarized affidavit instead. I looked up an attorney in South Africa that is a Notary of Public. I booked an appointment with him and written a statement in front of him about my personal status before marriage. The attorney completed and signed the necessary Notary documentation, disclaiming that I have written and signed the statement in front of him. I sent this document with my agent to the High Court of Pretoria to have it Apostilled.

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from South Africa

Name in South Africa: Police Clearance Certificate (“PCC”)

**How to obtain?**

- Certified copy of ID or passport
- Set of fingerprints obtained from your local police station (example of SAPS-91a form)
- SAPS application form
- Applicants outside of RSA can also be assisted by submitting their applications via courier and doing their fingerprints at their local police station or embassy

Agents can be used in South Africa to assist with the application of a PCC and this can be done while the applicant is in South Africa or abroad. The advantage of using an agent is that they follow up weekly on your applications thus aiding in your application being expedited.

A PCC is valid for 6 months from date of issue.

This should be planned carefully as waiting periods on a PCC is currently between 8 -12 weeks and Apostille a further 6 – 8 weeks.

**Cost**: **R 150.00** (excluding courier services if applying from abroad and excluding agent costs)

[**Link**](https://www.dpcc.co.za/police-clearance/)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in South Africa

**How to obtain?**

You can visit DIRCO at 460 Soutpansberg Road, Rietondale, Pretoria at no cost or you can use an agent.

Agents can be used to assist with the Apostille of documentation through DIRCO. 

The current waiting period is around 6 – 8 weeks at DIRCO.

**Cost: free**

[**Link**](http://www.dirco.gov.za/)
