## Audit Committee Member

**\*This is an official public position, members are listed in the association’s list of functionaries\***  
This position is relevant for Israelis only.

**Scope**: small scope.

**Details**: Every nonprofit organization is Israel must appoint at least 2 members to the organization’s audit committee. The committee’s duty is to make sure everything in the organization is done appropriately. These are the responsibilities of the committee:

- Check the integrity of the association’s operations and its institutions, check the proper management of the association, savings, efficiency, effectiveness and ethicality.
- Make sure the association is working towards achieving its official goals efficiently and economically;
- Follow the implementation of the decisions of the general meeting and the board;
- Suggest to the board ways to correct deficiencies in management.
- Review the association’s financial affairs, its bookkeeping and payments, including the designation of the association’s funds to the promotion of its goals;
- Review the scope of the accountant’s work and his salary;
- Check any other issue related to the activity of the association.

Because we are talking about a brand new association, without any employees or big funds, the job of the audit committee is very minimal. It will be mostly required at the end of the year – to review some reports that are created by the board and the accountant. By law, the committee is required to hold only one meeting per year (can be an online meeting).

**Being a functionary in a registered association is a real prestigious position that people are fighting for. Now, it could be you!**

**Requirements**:

1. Israeli citizenship
2. Native Hebrew
3. Excellent writing ability
4. Not afraid of legal jargon and numbers 🙂
5. Honestly cares about the association, its goals and its success

**Wanna join the team?** Send us an email with your CV and an explanation about yourself, your interest in this project and your availability to [info@aic.org.il](mailto:info@aic.org.il), with the title “Audit Committee application”.
