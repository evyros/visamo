# Official Documentation from Chile

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

The documents required in order to apply for the gradual process are obtain at the “Registro Civil”  (The Chilean National Registration Office) with the exception of the “Personal Status Certificate” which will be explained below.

## Birth Certificate from Chile

Name in Chile: “Certificado de Nacimiento Para Todo Tramite”  (In English: : Birth Certificate for all procedures”)

**How to obtain?**

This document can be easily obtained either at any branch of the Registro Civil or online at the website of the Registro Civil. Bear in mind that the document will have an online signature regardless if it is obtained in walks-in persons at the branch of the Registro Civil or through their website. Also bear in mind that the website is only available in Spanish.

**Cost:** No Charges and it can be required as many time as needed.

[**Link**](https://www.registrocivil.cl/principal/servicios-en-linea)

## Name change certificate from Chile

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Under no circumstance a Chilean national can change his/her surname to any surname he wants or adopt his/her partner’s surname. Chile is very strict regarding the surname and name changes therefore is very unlikely that this type of certificate would be required. Nonetheless, under Law 21.334 adopted in 2021, Chilean nationals can – for one time only – request the change of the order of their surnames having their mother’s surname first and their father’s surname second

Name in Chile: “Certificado de Cambio de Nombre de Apellidos”

**How to obtain?**

This certificate can only be requested at any of the branches nationwide of the Registro Civil

**Cost:** No charges as April 2022

## Personal status certificate from Chile

Under Chilean Legislation there is no such thing as a certificate of singleness. There is in the Registro Civil system a Marriage Certificate and a “Civil Partnership” Certificate (In Spanish “Acuerdo de Union Civil’). Unfortunately for the Registro Civil Authorities – and the Chilean Legislation – if you are not in the system of the Registro Civil files as married or under a Civil Partnership you are automatically single and therefore there is no document that they can provide. No matter how much you try, they will not produce any certificate for this purpose.

Name in Chile: Declaracion Jurada De Solteria (In English: “Affidavit of Singleness”) / Declaracion Jurada De Solteria Previo a Matrimonio (In English: “Affidavit for single status prior to being married”)

**How to obtain?**

As explained above, the Chilean Authorities do not issue a Single Certificate, it simply does not exist under Chilean legislation. But it is possible to do an Affidavit at a Notary stating that you are single. Bear in mind that it is required to have two witnesses for this procedure.

About **previous** personal status: This is a bit tricky since most of the clerks at notaries in Chile will not have done this before. There is not a specific format for it and they do not know what to write. Therefore, you should come to the notary and tell them that you need an Affidavit in which you are stating that you were single until the date of your wedding. The affidavit should have your personal information and the information of your spouse including his/her passport number or his/her Teudat Zeut. You do not need the spouse to be present when you make the affidavit but you do need the wedding certificate as a proof. Also, you will need two witnesses for this affidavit.

Considerations: PIBA is aware of this situation regarding the non existence of a Single Certificate and they accept the Affidavit. It is accepted in different branches and it has been accepted always. Nonetheless many of the clerks of the different branches of the  PIBA are not aware of this situation, and they will demand to see a Certificate of Singleness. Please be patient and explain to them the inexistence of this type of certificate in Chile.

 **Cost:** The charges vary depending on the Notary

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Chile

Name in Chile: Certificado de Antecedentes Penales Para Fines Especiales (Certificate of Criminal Records for special purpose)

**How to obtain?**

This document can be easily obtained either at any branch of the Registro Civil or online at the website of the Registro Civil. For the online process you will need to use your “Clave Unica” (the Chilean personal password), which can be obtained at any branch of the Registro Civil.Bear in mind that the document will have an online signature regardless if it is obtained in walk-in persons at the branch of the Registro Civil or through their website. Also bear in mind that the Registro Civil website is only available in Spanish.

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Chile

**How to obtain?**

The Apostille in Chile is quite an easy process. You need to go with the documents printed and present them at the branches. Bear in mind that in Chile there are specific places to do apostille for document issued in the different public offices.

1. For documents obtained at the Registro Civil you have to apostille them at a branch of the Registro Civil. It does not have to be on the branch of your residence city.
2. Metropolitan Region of Santiago (in Spanish: Region Metropolitana): it can only be done at the Bicentenario Registro Civil branch located in Moneda 1342, Santiago, Región Metropolitana, Chile. Walks-in are not allowed nowadays so you need to get an appointment online. Appointments can be made at the website of Registro Civil ([https://agenda.qa.registrocivil.cl/](https://agenda.qa.registrocivil.cl/)) and it is required to have a valid Chilean ID to do this. Bear in mind that you should ask for an appointment with time in advance since there is the only place to do it in Santiago and there are not many spaces available. Also bear in mind that your appointment is linked to your Chilean National Id Number (in Spanish RUT) so no one can do this procedure under your name

- Chilean Regions: In the regions this can be done at the specific branch in the Region Capital City (For Example in the Maule Region, it has to be done at the main branch of Talca and not at other cities branches). Walk-ins are accepted so you do not need to ask for an appointment. Since you do not need an appointment, someone else can do the apostille procedure for you without the need of a power of attorney.

- For the **affidavits** you have to apostille them at a branch of the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights (in Spanish Ministerio de Justicia y Derechos Humanos). As April of 2022, there is only one branch available in the country located in Moneda 1155, piso 1, Santiago Centro. Walks-in are available and you do not need an appointment. Also, someone else can do the apostille procedure for you without the need of a power of attorney.

**Cost:** Apostille in the Registro Civil and in the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights is free of charge.
