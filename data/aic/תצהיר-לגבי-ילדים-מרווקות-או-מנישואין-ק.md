# תצהיר לגבי ילדים מרווקות או מנישואין קודמים, ואפוטרופסות על הילדים / Affidavit about children from previous singleness period or previous marriage, and guardianship of children

The *affidavit about children from previous singleness period or previous marriage, and guardianship of children*, is an affidavit that is sometimes required by PIBA (the Population and Immigration Authority) in [Partner Visa](https://aic.org.il/the-gradual-process-birds-eye-view/) processes. **It is used as a way to prove the foreign partner immigrating to Israel does not have children, and is not a legal guardian of any children.**

An affidavit is a legal document, and **giving a false affidavit is a punishable criminal offense.** Before proceeding, learn more information about [affidavits in Israel](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/).

The Israeli Association for International Couples has created bilingual, pre-structed, lawyer-reviewed affidavit formats, for the personal use of international couples in Israel.
