# תצהיר מצב אישי (קודם ועדכני) / Affidavit of personal status (Current and Previous)

The *Affidavit of Personal Status (Current and Previous)*, is an affidavit that is sometimes required by PIBA (the Population and Immigration Authority) in [Partner Visa](https://aic.org.il/the-gradual-process-birds-eye-view/) processes. **It is used to prove the current personal status and/or previous personal status of a foreign partner who applies to immigrate to Israel.**

An affidavit is a legal document, and **giving a false affidavit is a punishable criminal offense.** Before proceeding, learn more information about [affidavits in Israel](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/).

The Israeli Association for International Couples has created bilingual, pre-structed, lawyer-reviewed affidavit formats, for the personal use of international couples in Israel.
