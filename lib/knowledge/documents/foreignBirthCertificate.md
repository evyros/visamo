# Birth certificate (תעודת לידה)

The original, from the country of birth.

The birth certificate of the foreign partner, the original, from the country
of birth; 2 copies (§ד.2.ה; AS/6).

- Original birth certificates from the former USSR issued up to 1998 need no
  authentication (§ד.2.ה). See [certification.md](certification.md).
