# Letters of recommendation (מכתבי המלצה)

At least 3 letters in Hebrew from Israeli family and friends about your relationship, each with a copy of the writer's Teudat Zehut and their contact details.

Recommendation letters are letters written by family members and friends of
the couple. They're used as proof that the relationship between the Israeli
partner and the foreign partner is real and honest: an honest relationship
is the basis on which the foreign partner can get legal status in Israel.
The procedure asks for letters from family and friends about how you met,
each with a copy of the writer's ID and their contact details (5.2.0008
§ד.2.ח).

## How to get it

Ask Israeli family members and friends who know you as a couple to write
one, in Hebrew. Each writer signs their letter, and gives you a copy of
their Teudat Zehut to attach.

## What it should include

- **At least 3 letters,** from different writers. It's recommended to ask
  different kinds of people: family members, close and further, and friends
  from different circles.
- **From Israelis:** family members or friends who are Israeli.
- **In Hebrew.**
- **Who the writer is,** and how they know you.
- **What they know of your relationship:** how you met, how long you've been
  together, and what they've seen of your life together.
- **Usually about 3 paragraphs long.** The shorter your relationship, or
  the less other proof you have that you live together, the more detailed
  the letters should be in order to build a strong file.
- **The writer's signature and the date.**
- **The writer's contact details,** and **a copy of their Teudat Zehut**,
  with the letter.
- **If you haven't lived together:** the letters should say that the writer
  saw you together. They're part of the stronger case you'll need (see
  [documents.md](../documents.md)).
