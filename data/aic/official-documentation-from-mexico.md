# Official Documentation from Mexico

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Mexico

Name in Mexico: Acta de Nacimiento Certificada

**How to obtain?**

This document can be request in “Registro Civil Municipal” offices where you were register or in “Dirección General del Registro Civil” offices in any State of the country (this just apply when you are living in a different city to your home town).

In both options you need to ask for “Acta de Nacimiento para Apostilla” (This is very important).

Remember that for apostille you need to bring documents with an autograph signature so that’s why birth certificates from a machine or requested online won´t be accepted. The time to get it is usually at the moment or a day after.

Important note: Is not possible to find online the requirements to get “Acta de Nacimiento para Apostilla” so you need to call or send an e-mail to your nearest office; but usually they ask for the same requirements that a regular birth certificate.

**Cost:** Depends each Estate, **between 100 to 250 Mexican pesos.**

**Link:** In the link below you will find the main “Registro Civil” offices per State or you can search the website of your municipality.

[**Link**](https://embamex.sre.gob.mx/argentina/images/consular/ofregcivilenrepubmexic)

## Name change certificate from Mexico

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

This process does not exist in Mexico.

By Law, all citizens have right to have a personal identity and that includes main name and two last names (From father and mother). In Mexico, to be married is not an excuse to change your name. In case that you decide to use your husband’s name abroad you can do it but for all Mexican process you need to use your full name as is written in your birth certificate.

There is a chance to put your husband´s name in your passport and will be included as an annotation (it means that your name will appear as your birth certificate as usual). For this process you need to renew your passport (more information in link below).

**Cost:** Depends for how long you want your passport valid and costs change every year.

**[Link](https://www.gob.mx/tramites/ficha/renovacion-de-pasaporte-a-personas-mayores-de-edad/SRE113)**

## Personal status certificate from Mexico

Name in Mexico: Constancia de Inexistencia de Registro de Matrimonio / Constancia de Soltería.

**How to obtain?**

The State Government is in charge to provide this document. You can request it in “Dirección General del Registro Civil” offices in any State the country. It’s important to mention them that you need this document for apostille.

The requirements are different in any State and usually takes from 30 minutes to 10 days to complete the process, and also, the document can be valid between 30 to 180 days.

Note: I suggest to call or send an e-mail to the office where you will request the document before to go (e.g. In Queretaro they don’t mention in their website that you need a birth certificate and also a motivation letter).

**Cost:** Depends the State but it cost between **100 to 650 Mexican pesos.**

In the following link you will find the addresses and website per State. **[Link](https://embamex.sre.gob.mx/argentina/images/consular/ofregcivilenrepubmexic)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Mexico

Name in Mexico: Constancia de Antecedentes Penales Federales

**How to obtain?**

The “Órgano Administrativo Desconcentrado Prevención y Readaptación Social” is in charge to provide this document and you or someone else can do it only in Mexico City. The only way to be able to get the document is to prove you need it for a process abroad, so if you are currently living in Mexico you need to send an e-mail to the Israel´s embassy in Mexico asking for a letter and add a copy of your passport. This process can take between 1 to 2 weeks. So if in this time they don’t reply ask your partner to call and chose the Hebrew line and you will get it quickly.

You will need also a birth certificate (acta de nacimiento certificada) and your passport or ID. Once you have these three documents you need to scan them and submitted them to get an appointment (usually takes 2 weeks to get it). You need to print the appointment’s confirmation and “Manifiesto de decir Verdad”, and “Formato e5cinco” (the last one to make the payment).

Once you have the appointment try to be there 30 minutes before, there’s a big line and people go in small groups and don´t forget to bring a pen with you, you will need it.

If it’s possible, go with someone because you can’t bring your phone with you to the office.

The process in the office takes around 1.5 hours so I recommend to make the apostille process another day.

\*Note: If you are doing the process in Israel you need to contact The Mexican Embassy.

**Cost: $197 Mexican pesos**

**Links:**

Email of the Israeli Embassy in Mexico:[Consular3@mexico.mfa.gov.il](mailto:Consular3@mexico.mfa.gov.il)

To get all the requirements and to ask for an appointment: **[Link](https://www.gob.mx/prevencionyreadaptacion/articulos/tramita-tu-constancia-de-antecedentes-penales?idiom=es)**

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Mexico

**How to obtain?**

There are two authorities in charge of this process: The State apostille and the Federal one. In the State Authority you will apostille your Birth Certificate and your Personal Status Certificate. These documents must be from the same State than the Apostille branch (for more information check the link below).

The only document that you need to apostille in the Federal Authority is the Certificate on Non-Criminal records. This office is located only in Mexico City. For this process you do not need an appointment and the process takes around 3 hours. You will need to pay in a bank before you go, so be sure to pay a day before and try to be in the office around 9am.

If you do not want to travel to Mexico City for this process, there is an option to send the document by postal mail or someone else can do it for you.

**Cost:** Federal apostille **$1743 Mexican pesos per document**; State apostille: Depends the State.
