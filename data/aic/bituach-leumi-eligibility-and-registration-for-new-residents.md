# Bituach Leumi Eligibility and Registration for New Residents

Did you just become an Israeli resident? Congratulations! We’ve got you covered.

**The Israeli Association for International Couples** (R.A.), in collaboration with the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi), is proud to share the first-ever official and confirmed guide for new residents on how to register with Bituach Leumi.

In this article, you’ll find a clear step-by-step guide to the registration process, along with information on who’s eligible for Bituach Leumi services. It’s especially helpful for those in the partner visa process who’ve just received their A5 visa, as well as other new residents navigating the system.

To go straight to eligibility [click here](https://aic.org.il/#eligibility)!

To go straight to the registration instructions [click here](https://aic.org.il/#instructions)!

## What is “Bituach Leumi” (National Insurance)?

The National Insurance Institute (המוסד לביטוח לאומי) is the body entrusted with the realization of a significant part of social rights in Israel. According to the law, every Israeli resident aged 18 or older **must** be insured with National Insurance and pay insurance premiums (apart from the populations that are exempt from payment). The National Insurance premiums are calculated according to the income of the insured (from work or otherwise) and according to their insurance status as an employee, self-employed, or unemployed.

Individuals registered with the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) are entitled to state health insurance. This coverage is provided by the State of Israel to eligible residents, subject to the payment of monthly health insurance premiums to Bituach Leumi, unless an exemption applies. Healthcare services are delivered through one of four Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs), known in Hebrew as *Kupot Holim*.

In addition, National insurance policyholders may be entitled to coverage and receive allowances in certain cases in accordance with the law. Among the allowances, you can find disability allowance, senior citizen’s allowance, survivors allowance, unemployment benefits, maternity benefits, child benefits, income support, and more.

## Who is eligible to receive the National Insurance services?

The National Insurance services are provided to those who are residents of Israel – Israeli citizens, Israeli permanent residents, and Israeli temporary residents. According to the definition of the National Insurance, an “Israeli resident” is a person whose center of life is in Israel, and whose absence from Israel is only a temporary absence.

Important! One cannot opt out of National Insurance. National Insurance is mandatory for all Israeli residents. **An Israeli resident that does not pay National Insurance premiums may be accumulating debt**.

According to Section 2א of the [National Insurance Law](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/039_002.htm), **holders of visa types A1, A2, A5, and A4** (accompanying A2) are recognized as Israeli residents for the purpose of the National Insurance under two cumulative conditions:

1. **A minimum period of stay of 183 days in Israel** – starting in 2018, the number of 183 days will also take into account legal stay during the period of six months preceding the receipt of the resident visa.
2. **Their center of life is in Israel**.

In this article, we will focus on partners of Israelis who received A5 “general temporary” status as part of the gradual partner visa process, the procedure of determining their residency by the National Insurance and receiving their rights as residents of Israel.

**In most cases, foreign partners of Israelis who received an A5 visa have already completed the minimum period of stay (condition 1), and are therefore entitled to be considered residents immediately and without delay, subject to the completion of the residency determination procedure as detailed below.**

**Important:** Holding an A1, A2, A4, or A5 visa does *not* automatically qualify a person as a “resident of Israel” under the National Insurance Institute’s definition for residency.

### Before starting the registration process

Before starting the registration and residency determination process, please note the following:

1. Apply for a **summary of entries and exits from the country from PIBA (“בירור פרטים על נוסע – כניסות ויציאות מהארץ**“). It is recommended to apply for it in person (no appointment is needed). Please note that we did encounter cases in which a Bituach Leumi application was approved without it.
2. **Activate your biometric ID** – your biometric ID should be with you and activated in order for BL to find your data correctly in their system. However, please note that in some cases (specifically when a **temporary ID** has been issued), the BL registration process can begin before activation of the biometric ID.

## Procedure for determining residency and registration to National Insurance

### Step 1: Form BL/625 – Questionnaire to determine the residency of temporary resident visa holders

  
Print and fill out the [BL/625 form](https://www.btl.gov.il/%D7%98%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9D%20%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/forms/insurance_forms/Pages/625%20-%20%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9F%20%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%AA%20%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA%20-%20%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%99%20%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%94%20%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%91%20%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%A2%D7%99.aspx) from the National Insurance website. The person filling out the form is the new resident, the foreign spouse. The form is in Hebrew only and must be filled out in Hebrew.

The form is divided into 10 parts:

1. Personal details and contact details
    - “Date of entry into Israel” – specify the date on which you moved to Israel as the main place of residence.
    - Spouse details – If you’re a married couple, include the name and ID number of your partner.
2. Foreign citizenship – fill in the passport number, date of issue of your A5 visa and its validity, and specify periods of previous stay on visas B1 – B2. These periods will be counted towards the 183 days that are required for the beginning of eligibility.
3. Place of residence
4. Details of children and the educational institutions they attend
5. Details of Israeli Bank account
6. Details of a vehicle you own (when applicable)
7. Purpose of stay in Israel – in the case of international couples, settling in Israel is for the purpose of family reunification. In the case of married couples, you can mark “marriage”. In the case of non-married couples (common-law partners/ “Yeduim Batzibur”), you can put down “איחוד משפחות” / “ידועים בציבור” / “זוגיות”.
8. Occupation
9. Choosing an HMO
10. Additional data and statement – as part of the additional data, you can briefly explain how the center of your life is expressed in Israel.

### Step 2: Attaching center of life documents

As stated above, the granting of A5 status by the Population Authority does not automatically establish recognition as a “resident of Israel” for the purpose of National Insurance. Therefore, you must fill out a residency determination questionnaire as well as present proofs and references accordingly.

The center of life documents required by the National Insurance are often less stringent than those required by PIBA in the gradual process. Assuming that you recently submitted a request to upgrade your status to an A5 visa, your center of life documents should be ready for submission to the National Insurance as well.

Below is the list of documents to attach:

1. Photocopy of a foreign passport
2. Photocopy of A5 visa
3. Copy of previous visas from the time of entry to Israel until receiving the A/5 visa
4. Summary of entries and exits from the country from the Population Authority
5. Apartment rental contract – if the contract is in the name of the Israeli spouse, you can attach a letter from the apartment owner about the couple’s joint residence in the apartment.
6. Confirmation of ownership of an Israeli bank account
7. If you have children in educational institutions – confirmation of the children’s registration in the educational institution
8. If you own a vehicle – a vehicle license in your name
9. Employment contract in Israel (when applicable)
10. 6 recent payslips (for the self-employed – “Osek Patur” / “Osek Murshe” certificate) (when applicable)
11. Any other document showing that the center of your life is in Israel (registration to academic studies, registration to Hebrew studies, Israeli driver’s license, registration of dog ownership, etc.)  

### Step 3: Sending the request

According to the instructions of the Insurance Division, the residency determination questionnaire and the attached documents should be submitted through the website or through a service box at the National Insurance branch.

- **Submission through the website**: Upload clear scans of the completed and signed form 625 and the attached documents to the [online document submission form](https://b2b.btl.gov.il/BTL.ILG.Payments/DocumentsInfo.aspx).
    - Select: “מחלקת גבייה ממבוטחים”.
    - Fill in personal details in Hebrew only
    - Select the documents to upload and send
- **Filing in the box at the branch:** Go to the National Insurance branch and insert the documents in the service box of the insurance department (מחלקת גבייה וביטוח) at any time of the day. Make sure all documents are firmly stapled together, and write your ID number at the top of every page.

Regardless of the way you chose to submit your application, register to the [Personal Service](https://ps.btl.gov.il/#/QrCode/Registration/user/) of the National Insurance website to receive updates on your application.

### Step 4: Determining residency

After uploading the documents through the website, the documents will be forwarded to a clerk for examination. If there’s anything missing, the National Insurance clerk will contact you by phone or by letter. The National Insurance letters are sent through Israel Post to your registered address and simultaneously appear in the personal service area of the [National Insurance website](https://www.btl.gov.il/Pages/default.aspx) (under “Letters”).

If your application was complete, without any deficiencies and Israeli residency has indeed been determined, you will receive a notification from the National Insurance through a text message and a letter (to the personal area on the website and through Israel Post). If you were not able to register for the personal area before, after receiving the notification about the determination of residency, you can register for the personal area on the National Insurance website.

### Step 5: Administrative processing at the HMO

Once the process of determining the residency is complete, go to a branch of your selected HMO with your Israeli ID (the selection is made as part of form 625) for administrative admission and to receive a magnetic membership card.

It is possible that the office worker at your HMO will ask you if you would like to purchase additional coverage for a monthly payment (שב”ן – שירותי בריאות נוספים). These plans are private, secondary insurance plans that will give you additional coverage for things that aren’t covered under public health insurance.

Once the administrative processing at the HMO is done and you have your magnetic card, you can start receiving health services as an Israeli resident!
