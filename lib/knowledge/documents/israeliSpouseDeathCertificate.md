# Death certificate of a previous spouse (תעודת פטירה של בן/בת זוג קודם/ת)

For each of the Israeli partner's previous marriages that ended in the spouse's death.

For each of the Israeli partner's previous marriages that ended in the
spouse's death: the previous spouse's death certificate (תעודת פטירה).

## How to get it

### A death registered in Israel

From the Population and Immigration Authority, free. As the spouse, you can
ask for it yourself:

- **Online,** a digitally signed certificate, right away, from your personal
  area on the government's site (gov.il).
- **By request form,** online, by fax or by mail: the certificate is sent to
  your address in the Population Registry.

It's an Israeli document, in Hebrew: no certification or translation.

### A death registered abroad

From the civil registry of the country where the death was registered, like
the foreign partner's previous spouse's death certificate (see it, by
country). It's certified there: an apostille, or consular legalization if
that country isn't in the Apostille Convention (see
[certification.md](../certification.md)).

## What it should include

- **One for each marriage** that ended in the spouse's death.
- **For a death abroad:** the certification of that country, and a certified
  translation unless it's in Hebrew or Arabic. In practice, an English
  document is usually accepted without one.

## Questions

**Where do I get my late spouse's death certificate?**
If the death was registered in Israel, from the Population and Immigration
Authority: download a digitally signed certificate from your personal area
on gov.il, or send the request form. It's free.
