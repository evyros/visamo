# Official Documentation from Austria

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Austria

Austrian name: Geburtsurkunde/ internationale Geburtsurkunde/Österreich

**How to obtain?**

The birth certificate & international birth certificate are issued by your local **r**egistry office (Standesamt).  
Necessary documents: passport or ID  
In person: An appointment is required, it can be done via phone, email.  
Online: You have to have a valid online signature (Status 2021).

**Cost: 7,20 €**  (costs can vary a little bit)

Links: [Info about the issuing of a birth certificate](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/familie_und_partnerschaft/geburt/3/1/Seite.085100.html#AllgemeineInformationen), [Contact list of all registry offices in Vienna](https://www.wien.gv.at/advuew/internet/AdvPrSrv.asp?Layout=stelle&Type=K&stellecd=2006061211245513&STELLE=Y)

## Personal status certificate from Austria

Austrian name: Teilauszug gemäß §58 PStG 2013 über das Bestehen einer Ehe oder einer eigetragenen Partnerschaft / Personenstandsurkunde

**How to obtain?**

The personal status certificate is issued by your local **registry office (Standesamt).  
**Necessary documents: passport or ID

**Cost: 8-12 €**  (costs can vary a little bit)  
The Info Link is not working, but both documents (personal status & birth certificate) can be usually done at the same time at your local registry office.

### Can these certificates also be issued as “international certificates”?

This is possible for the following documents: Birth, marriage, death and partnership certificates (Section 53 (4) PStG 2013)

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Austria

**Austrian name:** Strafregisterbescheinigung

**How to obtain?**

The Non-criminal record is issued by the State Police Headquarters (Landespolizei Direktion)  
Necessary documents: passport or ID  
In person: An appointment is required, it can be done via phone, email.  
Online: You have to have a valid online signature

**Cost: 25 €**

**Link:** [**Police Headquarters**](https://www.polizei.gv.at/wien/buergerservice/strafregister/start.aspx) (at the top menu bar you can pick your province/region):

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Austria

**How to obtain?**

**Non-criminal record:** The Apostille for the **Non-criminal** **record** is issued at the Ministry of Interior (Innenministerium) and their legal office. An appointment is required, calling them is the most convenient way.

**Necessary documents:** passport or ID + the documents you want the Apostille for  
**Cost: 17,50 Euro**  (Apostille + processing fees)

**Link:** [**Ministry of Interior**](https://www.bmi.gv.at/) (call the regular number and tell them, that you need an Apostille for the non criminal record, you will be forwarded and will get an appointment)

B**irth certificate & the personal status:**  
The Apostilles for the **birth certificate & the personal status** are issued at the town hall (Rathaus). An appointment is not needed (in Vienna) you can go there between 8.00-12.00

Please note that it can be handled differently during Corona and you might need an appointment.

**Necessary documents:** passport or ID + the documents you want the Apostille for  
**Cost: 17,50 Euro**  (Apostille + processing fees**)**

**Link (Vienna)**: [**Town hall**](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtshelfer/dokumente/urkunden/standesamt/apostille.html)
