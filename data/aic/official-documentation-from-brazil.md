# Official Documentation from Brazil

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

## Birth Certificate from Brazil

Name in Brazil: Certidão de Nascimento de inteiro teor.

**How to obtain?**

Call or go to the registry office, preferably where your registration was made when you were born, but you can apply from a different registry office. Apply for a full-length birth certificate.  It takes up to 5 to 30 working days for the certificate to be available. Usually, if you’re going where your registration was first made, it will be faster.

Note that the online birth certificate may not be accepted by the Israeli government.

**Cost: Start at R$70,00** depending on each state.

## Name change certificate from Brazil

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in Brasil: Certidão de casamento / Certidão de casamento averbada (When divorced)

**How to obtain?**

Usually, this kind of change happens after marriage, and this will be stated on your Marriage certificate. If you’re divorced, this will be stated on your marriage certificate with the annotation of the divorce.

Call or go to the registry office, preferably where your registration was made when you got married, but you can apply from a different registry office. Apply for a Marriage certificate.

**Cost: Start at R$40,00** depending on each stated and how many annotations are on it.

## Personal status certificate from Brazil

Name in Brazil: Declaração pública de estado civil / certidão negativa de casamento.

**How to obtain?**

There are two options for this document:

Negative marriage certificate (cheaper): Go to the registry office where your birth was registered and request a negative marriage certificate, preferably from the year of your birth. This certificate is a search made in all the registry offices in the Brazilian territory where it will be verified if any marriage registration was made in your name during the requested period.

Public declaration of marital status (more expensive): Go to the registry office where your birth or marriage was registered and request a public declaration of marital status. Two witnesses must be present.

**Cost: from R$17,00 to R$550,00**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Brazil

Name in Brazil: Certificado de antecedentes criminais

**How to obtain?**

This is requested online on the federal police website or on the government website, you just need to access the link and fulfill the requested information. Some registry does the recognition of the certificated under a small fee (up to R$5,00), others say that this recognition is not necessary anymore as the document issued has an electronic key, so it’s up to you to do the recognition or not.

**Cost: free**

[**Link**](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/emitir-certidao-de-antecedentes-criminais)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Brazil

**How to obtain?**

Go to any registry with the original documents and ask for Haia apostille. This takes up to 7 to 15 working days depending on the registry.

**Cost: from to R$39,58 to $124,27 each document.** Depends on the state.
