# AIC’s First Beach Event Becomes One of Our Biggest Events Yet! [Gallery]

What is better than the Israeli beach in the summer? An AIC summer party at the beach!  
After seeing you all in the past AIC events, we couldn’t wait too long to bring our community back together.

On July 27th, we invited international couples and their families to gather in Hof Hatzuk in Tel Aviv, to enjoy the end of the week in front of the beautiful sunset. AIC created the perfect beach chill-zone with a fresh fruits bar, snacks, drinks, popsicles, and even pizzas! Our beach party also turned into one of our biggest events yet, with over 130 participants from all over the world – Find yourself in our photo gallery below!

Apart from enjoying the beach, we loved seeing couples and their families mingling and getting to know one another. We had first-timers, who just found out about our events, as well as returning couples who just waited for the next AIC event! It was especially wonderful to see international kids meeting other international kids, all while enjoying the beautiful weather and the great company of our beautiful community.

Once again we thank AIC’s events team for all their hard work and dedication. With a minimal event donation, donations from the community and volunteers’ support, AIC was able to create an amazing event. To allow us to continue hosting events and fostering bonds within our community, [please donate to AIC today](https://aic.org.il/donations/)!

Missed the event or couldn’t make it? [Subscribe to our newsletter](https://aic.org.il/subscription-to-newsletter/) to get notified first and never miss a great time again!

See you at the next event!
