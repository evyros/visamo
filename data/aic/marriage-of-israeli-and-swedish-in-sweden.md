# Marriage of Israeli and Swedish in Sweden

Below you can find a description of the procedure for a civil wedding in Sweden, between an Israeli citizen and a Swedish citizen.

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

There are two types of marriage in Sweden, civil and religious and both are accepted by Israel. In case of a religious wedding, one person needs to be a member of the Swedish church (or other religious community). The minister will then provide you with a marriage certificate.

## Documents needed for the Israeli side

- Document stating you are not married or proving your marital status from Misrad Hapnim in English.

Israel doesn’t explicitly issue a “single status certificate” or a “certificate of non-impediment” (“nulla osta”) which is required by most EU countries in order to marry. However, Sweden accepts the Israeli “equivalent” which is the “Population Registry Extract” (תמצית רישום).

A Population Registry Extract for Israelis can be issued at no cost by filing an [online request](https://www.gov.il/he/service/summaryinfofromthepopulationregistrylisting). After the request is submitted, the certificate is mailed to the address registered in the person’s identity card. It took about two weeks for the certificate to arrive by mail. You can request it also in English so no need for translation.

Another way to issue the Registry Extract is via one of PIBA’s self-service kiosks. Those can be found in most PIBA offices around Israel. This service is only available to Israelis who have a biometric ID. For verification purposes, the applicant must have their personal PIN or a personal credit card when applying at the kiosk.

## Documents needed from the Swedish side

In order to register it in Israel you need this: translated and apostille stamp on the marriage certificate and excerpt from the tax register showing you marital status and names. The marriage certificate is crucial here.

## Procedure (what does one have to do before, during, and after the marriage)

1. Hindersprövning. Download and fill out the forms found here and send it to Skatteverket (tax agency) . Application is free and takes up to a month to obtain. Make sure you do this in good time before your special day. You do this per mail. [https://skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/aktenskapochpartnerskap/forevigselnhindersprovning.4.76a43be412206334b89800020477.html](https://skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/aktenskapochpartnerskap/forevigselnhindersprovning.4.76a43be412206334b89800020477.html)  
    The documents from Israel need to be attached here to prove you are not married at the moment.
2. Decide on civil or religious wedding. Hand the Hindersprövning form to the person in charge of the wedding who will then send in the details to the Tax agency after the wedding. It takes about a week to get the marriage registered with the authorities in Sweden.
3. The minister or civil servant performing the wedding will give you marriage certificate.
4. Decide on a common or double surname. Can be found here and the application is free. Takes approx. 2 weeks to obtain and you do it per mail. [https://skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/namn/bytatilldinmakasellermakesefternamn.4.515a6be615c637b9aa44cb4.html](https://skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/namn/bytatilldinmakasellermakesefternamn.4.515a6be615c637b9aa44cb4.html)
5. Translate the certificate and place an apostille on it by a Notarius Publicus lawyer (cost about 350 SEK and up per apostille). Usually takes less than a week, but can take more depending on the translator, which also needs to be official from Kammarkollegiet. Check the list here of translators: [https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/vara-tjanster/oversattare/sok-oversattare-i-vart-register](https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/vara-tjanster/oversattare/sok-oversattare-i-vart-register)
6. Obtain a tax register form from Skatteverket. “Utdrag om folkbokföring 120 med alla relationer på engelska”. Get the same apostille on it.
7. Submit the documents to Misrad Hapnim.
