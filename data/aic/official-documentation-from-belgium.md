# Official Documentation from Belgium

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

\*This article is covering the procedure to obtain documentation in the region of **Flanders**. Please take into account that the described procedure may vary in other parts of Belgium.

## Birth Certificate from Belgium

Name in Belgium (Flanders): “Geboortecertificaat” / “Akte van geboorte”

**How to obtain?**

- **In person**: at the city council (gemeentebestuur) of your birth place, at the booth “Burgerzaken”
- **Online (with e-ID)**:
    - Through the online service (e-loket) of your city council
    - Through the page “[Mijn Burgerprofiel](https://www.vlaanderen.be/uw-overheid/mijn-burgerprofiel)” 

**Cost:** Free

**[Link](https://www.vlaanderen.be/akte-van-geboorte)** 

## Name change certificate from Belgium

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

Name in Belgium (Flanders): “Akte van naamsverandering”

**How to obtain?**

- **In person**: at the city council (gemeentebestuur) of the place where the name change was processed, at the booth “Burgerzaken”
- **Online (with e-ID)**:
    - Through the online service (e-loket) of your city council
    - Through the page “[Mijn Burgerprofiel](https://www.vlaanderen.be/uw-overheid/mijn-burgerprofiel)” 

**Cost:** Free

**[Link](https://www.vlaanderen.be/akte-van-naamsverandering)**

## Personal status certificate from Belgium

Name in Belgium (Flanders): “Burgerlijke staat” in het “Uittreksel uit het bevolkingsregister”  

**How to obtain?**

- **In person**: at the city council (gemeentebestuur) where you live, at the booth “Burgerzaken”
- **Online (with e-ID)**:
    - Through the online service (e-loket) of your city council
    - Through the page “[Mijn Burgerprofiel](https://www.vlaanderen.be/uw-overheid/mijn-burgerprofiel)” 
    - Through “[Mijn dossier](https://www.ibz.rrn.fgov.be/nl/rijksregister/mijn-dossier/)” of the national register (Rijksregister)

**Cost:** Free

**[Link](https://www.vlaanderen.be/uittreksel-uit-het-bevolkingsregister)**

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Belgium

Name in Belgium (Flanders): Uittreksel uit het strafregister

**How to obtain?**

- **In person**: at the city council (gemeentebestuur) where you live
- **Online (with e-ID)**:
    - Through the online service (e-loket) of your city council

**Cost:** Free

[**Link**](https://www.vlaanderen.be/uittreksel-uit-het-strafregister)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Belgium

**How to obtain?**

**— Online —**

For the following documents:

- Birth certificate
- Personal status certificate

It is possible to have the legalisation done automatically when requesting the documents online via the e-loket of the municipality. (Example for [Antwerp](https://www.antwerpen.be/info/5f843a4e132f5e33050b12e4/legalisatie-van-een-akte)) 

**— In person —**

Go to Brussels (Legalisatie bij de FOD Buitenlandse Zaken in Brussel) with your documents:

- [https://diplomatie.belgium.be/nl/Diensten/legalisatie\_van\_documenten](https://diplomatie.belgium.be/nl/Diensten/legalisatie_van_documenten)
- For appointments check this [link](https://appointment.diplomatie.be/Home/Index/50c4d0cf-0c53-4226-96be-d79fb262251f)

**Cost: 20 EUR** per document
