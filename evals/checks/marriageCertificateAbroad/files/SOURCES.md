# Where these files come from

philippines-real-apostille.jpg and philippines-real-certificate.jpg are a
real file, shared by the app's owner, as screenshots: a Philippine marriage
certificate (PSA copy, Municipal Form No. 97), a marriage in Marabut, Samar
on 3 June 2026, and the Department of Foreign Affairs' electronic apostille
for it (26 August 2026). For Israel, the Philippines issues both only as
files.
