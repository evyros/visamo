# Foreign passport (דרכון זר)

Valid for at least 2 more years, with copies of the main page, the visa pages and the border-control stamps.

The foreign partner's passport, valid for at least 2 more years (5.2.0008
§ד.2.ד), with copies of its pages (the AS/6 checklist).

## What it should include

- **Valid for at least 2 more years.**
- **Copies of the main page, the visa pages and the border-control
  stamps.** Bring the passport itself too: the clerk checks the copies
  against it.
- **If the foreign partner is outside Israel,** a copy is enough for the
  first appointment. They bring the passport itself after they arrive, when
  the office gives them the visa.

It needs no certification and no translation.

## If you don't have it

- **Valid for less than 2 years:** you can still apply. By the procedure,
  you're told in writing to renew it, and renewing it is a condition for
  getting the visa (5.2.0008 §ה.2(4)). Renewing it before you file saves
  that step.
- **No valid passport, while in Israel:** the foreign partner is referred
  to their country's embassy or consulate in Israel, to get one.

## Questions

**My partner's passport expires in a year and a half. Can we still file?**
Yes. The office tells you in writing to renew it, and the visa is given
only once the passport is valid for at least 2 more years. Renewing it
first saves the delay.

**Which pages do we copy?**
The main page with the photo, every page with a visa, and every page with a
border-control stamp.
