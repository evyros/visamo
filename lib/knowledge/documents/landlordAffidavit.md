# Landlord's affidavit (תצהיר של בעל/ת הדירה)

If you rent: an affidavit from your landlord in the ministry's wording (form AS/6), that they own the home and rent it to you, who else lives there, and who pays the bills. Signed in front of a lawyer, together with a scan of the landlord's Teudat Zehut.

If you rent: the landlord's affidavit, part of form AS/6, titled "affidavit
annexed to the lease – couples" (תצהיר נספח להסכם שכ"ד - בני זוג). The form
comes in two formats, with the pages in a different order: find it by its
title, not its page number. In it, the landlord declares that they own the home, that it's
rented to you, who else lives there, and who pays the bills. Not needed if
you own your home.

It's part of form AS/6, the application for married couples, so it's asked
for from married couples only. Like the lease, it's asked for when you live
together, or have lived together, and whenever the Israeli partner lives in
Israel.

## How to get it

The form is part of AS/6, on the Population and Immigration Authority's site
(see [sources.md](../sources.md)).

1. The landlord fills in the details (below).
2. They sign it **in front of a lawyer (עו"ד) or a Misrad Hapnim registrar
   (רשם)**, who confirms the declaration at the bottom of the page.
3. Attach **a scan of the landlord's Teudat Zehut**.

## What it should include

- **The landlord's details:** first name, last name and ID number (מ"ז).
- **Whose application it supports:** the tenant's name and ID number, and
  the application number and filing date, if you have them.
- **The home:** its address, that it's registered in the landlord's name
  (for a building with several homes, which one), and its size.
- **That it's rented to you,** and who else lives there: other families,
  floor by floor, with their names, ID numbers, relation, and who pays their
  rent.
- **Who pays each bill,** the landlord or you: electricity, water, phone and
  arnona, and how the payment is made.
- **The landlord's civil status,** number of children under 18, and
  occupation.
- **That since the lease was signed, you live only there,** and that the
  landlord will tell the Population and Immigration Authority if you leave.
- **The date, and the landlord's name and signature.**
- **The lawyer's or registrar's confirmation** (אישור קבלת ההצהרה): their
  name, license number and the date, the landlord's ID it was checked
  against, and their stamp and signature.
- **A scan of the landlord's Teudat Zehut**, with it.

## If you don't have it

In some cases, Misrad Hapnim waives the landlord's affidavit when there's a
strong reason you can't get it. Even so, it's strongly recommended to bring
it.
