# Shared bank account (חשבון בנק משותף)

A joint bank account confirmation. Without one, other proof of shared money: transfers to each other, card statements with household purchases or the same delivery address, receipts for large expenses.

A confirmation that you manage a joint bank account (אישור על ניהול חשבון
בנק משותף), from the AS/6 checklist. Misrad Hapnim (משרד הפנים) asks for it to show that
you share your finances. The procedure also lists a bank statement (תדפיס
חשבון בנק) among the proofs of a shared center of life (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ח).

It's part of form AS/6, so it's asked for from married couples. Common-law
couples bring bank statements instead.

## How to get it

From your bank: a confirmation of managing the account (אישור ניהול
חשבון), naming both of you as the account holders. Most banks let you
download it from their app or website; otherwise, ask at your branch. Add a
recent statement of the account.

## What it should include

- **Both your names** as the account's holders.
- **The bank's name and the account number.**
- **A recent statement** of the account, showing it's in use.
- **For an account abroad:** a certified translation unless it's in Hebrew
  or Arabic. In practice, English is usually accepted without one.

## If you don't have it

If you don't have a joint account, you'll have to show in other ways that
you share your money, and make a strong case for it. For example:

- transfers to each other;
- credit card statements showing household purchases, or deliveries to the
  same address;
- receipts for large shared expenses.
