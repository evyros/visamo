# Evidence of living together (הוכחות למגורים משותפים)

A shared lease or property, bills in both names, a joint bank account.

For every couple who say they live, or lived, together, married or not:
the evidence that you share a home. The procedure asks for proof of a shared
center of life for at least the last year, including a lease, shared bills
and bank statements (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ח).

Some of it is also asked for as its own document (the lease, the bills, the
joint bank account). Here, bring everything that shows you live together at
the same address.

## What it should include

- **Documents that put you both at the same address:** the lease or
  property in both your names, bills in both your names, statements of a
  joint bank account, and official mail addressed to each of you there.
- **Covering at least the last 12 months,** if you live together in Israel.
- **If you lived together abroad:** the same kinds of documents, from the
  home you shared there. A document that isn't in Hebrew or Arabic may need
  a translation (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## If you don't have it

Show in other ways that you run a household together: shared purchases,
receipts for things you bought for the home, and records of where you've
lived or stayed together (see [documents.md](../documents.md)).
