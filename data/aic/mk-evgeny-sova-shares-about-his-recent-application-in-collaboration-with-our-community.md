# MK Evgeny Sova Shares About Our Collaboration

MK Evgeny Sova (Yisrael Beytenu) shares a post on his official Facebook page about our recent shared effort of submitting a request for urgent discussion on the topic of the opening of the gradual process (you can read more about this move [here](https://aic.org.il/the-international-couples-community-and-the-gradual-process-brought-up-to-discussion-at-the-israeli-parliament/)).

This is a good moment to go show your support of MK Evgeny Sova on his recent post about the request he has made in collaboration with us! Not many parliament members are eager to speak about our community openly and publicly, and therefore we should encourage the ones that do.

Here’s what he said: “This week, I submitted a request for an urgent discussion in the Knesset’s Interior Committee on the issue of irrational waiting times for processing requests to regulate the status of foreign spouses. It is impossible to let people get married on the one hand and delay their lives and rights for many years on the other. Despite the apparent dispersion of the Knesset, there are issues that must be discussed and resolved”.
