# Health Insurance in Israel for B1 Visa Holders

Living in Israel with a B1 partner visa comes with unique challenges, and one crucial aspect is obtaining health insurance. B1 partner visa holders (like any B visa holder) are not considered Israeli residents by the Israeli National Insurance Institution (Bituach Leumi), and therefore are ineligible for public health insurance (eligibility for which begins when transitioning to an A5 visa).

For that reason, the only option for health insurance plan in Israel for B1 visa holders is private health insurance. To avoid confusion and misinformation on this topic we’d like to clarify that it doesn’t matter if you are insured through your employer, directly with an HMO or through an insurance company. It doesn’t matter if you have a magnetic card or not, or if you paid a full a “reduced” fee. A health insurance for a B1 visa holder will always be **private health insurance**.

In this article, you will find elaborate information about the available options of health insurance for non-residents – including special plans for partners of Israeli that offer attractive fees!

Wondering [why you’re seeing a “Bituach Leumi” deduction in your pay slip as a B1 holder](https://aic.org.il/why-am-i-paying-national-insurance-on-a-b1-visa/), even though you are ineligible for public health insurance? Check the article!

## Foreign Worker’s Health Insurance

The eligibility for foreign worker’s health insurance for B1 ***partner*** visa holders may be a complex matter due to the ambiguous legal situation.

The “Foreign Workers Law” states that an employer of a foreign worker must insure the worker with a health insurance that meets [certain standards](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%AA%D7%A0%D7%90%D7%99_%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97_%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93_%D7%96%D7%A8). Going plainly of off the law’s wording, the Foreign Workers Law does apply to B1 *partner visa* holders, and therefore the employer is obligated by law to insure their employees this way. However, a deeper look into the meaning of the law shows that it was not meant for the population of partners of Israelis, who are undergoing a naturalization process. For this reason and others, some employers may refuse to recognize that this law applies to partners of Israelis who have a B1 status.

Nevertheless, we advise all B1 partner visa holders to claim their right for a foreign worker’s insurance from their employer, as it is a good benefit that B1 visa holders technically qualify for by law.

It is important to note that individuals cannot purchase foreign worker’s health insurance for themselves; it can only be obtained by an employer. Moreover, as part of the legal arrangement, the employer is allowed to deduct up to a third of the insurance’s monthly cost from the employee’s salary, capped at a maximum of 134.46 NIS per month.

## Private Health Insurance in Israel for B1 Visa Holders

A private health insurance in Israel can be purchased from either an insurance company or directly from an Israeli Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) – Maccabi, Clalit, Meuhedet, or Leumit.

### Private Health Insurance via Insurance Company

There are several insurance companies in Israel that offer health insurance services for foreigners. These health insurance plans are often referred to as tourist health insurance, and the actual health services are usually provided through one of the four HMOs.

Notable examples of such insurance companies include [Ayalon](https://www.ayalon-ins.co.il/insurance/health/foreigns-emploies-and-turists/about/for-tourists/about/), [Harel (Tour & Care)](https://www.harel-group.co.il/Insurance/foreign/policies/Pages/tour-and-care.aspx), and [Menorah](https://www.menoramivt.co.il/general/health-insurance/tourist-top) (TOP Tourists).

### Private Health Insurance Directly from an HMO

Another option is to purchase health insurance directly from one of the four HMOs. The HMOs that offer a health insurance plan designed for non-Israeli residents are:

- Maccabi – the “[Maccabi Wellcome](https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/en/46562/main_english/our-healthcare-system/wellcome-healthcare-for-non-residents/)” plan,
- Meuhedet – the “[External Member](https://www.meuhedet.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97/%D7%AA%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A5/)” plan.
- Clalit – Special plan for partners of Israelis, read below.

**IMPORTANT**! Different private health insurance plans have different coverage and exclusions. Make sure the plan you get has the coverage you need!

### Health Insurance from Abroad – Travel Insurance

Additionally, B1 visa holders may consider obtaining health insurance from foreign companies, typically categorized as travel insurance. Such insurance can offer coverage for specific medical needs during the stay in Israel, but may prove less comfortable to maintain (as it would usually require the insured person to pay fully for private health services, and later making an indemnification claim).

## Health Insurance for Partners of Israeli Residents

Among the available private health insurance options, partners or spouses of Israelis have two unique options of **private** health insurance purchased directly from the HMO, usually at a lower cost. These option are exclusively offered by two of the Israeli HMOs: Clalit (כללית) and Meuhedet (מאוחדת), and **it is only available to partners or spouses of Israelis who are already insured with these specific HMOs.**

AIC has taken an active role in advocating for accessibility to these special insurance plans. We reached out to both Clalit and Meuhedet to clarify the registration procedures and address ongoing reports of challenges in accessing these plans due to uninformed clerks or unjust denial of registration.

After engaging with the headquarters of Clalit and Meuhedet, we have confirmed that partners of insured Israelis, whether married or unmarried, are indeed eligible to join these plans. AIC keeps working in collaboration with Clalit and Meuhedet on making these plans more easily available to the public. Please [contact our support email](https://aic.org.il/contact/) if you have any inquiries or if you faced any issue in the registration process.

### Clalit Partner Insurance – ביטוח לבן / בת זוג בכללית

Clalit offers a specific private plan tailored for partners of insured Israelis who aren’t considered residents and aren’t eligible for public health insurance.

The policy’s official name is “Insurance for Spouse of Insured Resident” – פוליסת נשוי לתושב

#### General conditions

- The Israeli partner must be a member of Clalit (under their public health insurance)
- Despite its name, the plan is available to both married and non-married partners
- Joining the plan is subject to Clalit’s approval and filling a health declaration
- Minimum duration of the insurance is **3 months**
- The monthly premium is differential and depends on the age of the joiner (as of April 2025):

#### How to join the Clalit Partner Insurance?

- Fill the [registration for](https://aic.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/%D7%91%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%95%D7%98%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%99-2023.pdf)[m](https://aic.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/%D7%91%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%95%D7%98%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%99-2023.pdf) (pages 3-4)
- Have the policy document (linked above) ready at hand, as some Clalit braches are still unfamiliar with the plan
- Go together to the “home branch” of Clalit of the Israeli partner
- Present proof of marriage/common-law relationship in **Hebrew**:
    - **Married couples** – Present a marriage certificate, translated to Hebrew with a notarized translation **OR** present the Israeli ID “Sefach” of the Israeli spouse that includes the personal details of the foreign spouse **OR** present a shared personal status [affidavit](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/) about your marriage status, validated by an Israeli lawyer.
    - **Non-married couples** – Present a [shared affidavit](https://aic.org.il/affidavits-in-israel/) about a common-law partnership (“Yeduim Batzibur” status), validated by an Israeli lawyer.

\*The policy details, conditions and pricing are under sole discretion of Clalit and may change. The information presented on this website is for convenience only and is not binding.

### Meuhedet “Partner Insurance” – ביטוח לבן / בת זוג במאוחדת

Meuhedet offers its regular [“External member” private insurance plan](https://www.meuhedet.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97/%D7%AA%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A5/) (that is available to all non-Israelis) at a reduced rate for partners of insured Israelis. Please check the full policy terms and exemptions on the linked Meuhedet website.

#### General conditions

- The Israeli partner must be a member of Meuhedet (under their public health insurance)
- The foreign partner can join regardless of their immgration status in Israel
- Joining the plan is subject to Meuhedet’s approval and filling a health declaration
- Minimum duration of the insurance is **4 months**
- The monthly premium is a flat rate of **160 NIS** (as of July 2023)
- The reduced fare is available only for applicants under 50 years old

***Note**:* While the Meuhedet website provides extensive information about the insurance policy, it does not mention the discounted rate for partners of insured Israelis. AIC has confirmed directly with Meuhedet that partners are eligible for a reduced monthly rate of 160 NIS.
