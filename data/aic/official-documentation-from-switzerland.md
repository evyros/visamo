# Official Documentation from Switzerland

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

\*This article is covering the procedure to obtain documentation in the canton of **Bern**. Please take into account that the described procedure may vary in other parts of Switzerland.

## Birth Certificate from Switzerland

Name in Switzerland: Extract from record of birth / Auszug aus dem Geburtsregister / Extrait de l acte de naissance

**How to obtain?**

Order the civil status certificate from the civil registry office in your place of birth. Check out this [link](https://www.e-service.admin.ch/competency-app/wicket/bookmarkable/ch.glue.suis.competency.app.pages.HeimatortPage?0) to determine your responsible office and check their process on their website or contact them via email. They have a very fast response time (1-3 days from my experience).

**Cost: 30CHF**

**[Link](https://www.zivilstand.sid.be.ch/de/start/dienstleistungen/bestellung-dokumente/geburtsurkunde.html)** (for Bern)

They probably can order it for you with an apostille for an additional fee. The total cost will probably be around **100CHF**. You can get the apostille for any document yourself at the cantonal chancellery (Staatskanzelei) or at the federal chancellery in Bern (see below).

## Name change certificate from Switzerland

\* Please note – Name change certificate is not necessary if you did not change your first / last name.

No information.

## Personal status certificate from Switzerland

Name in Switzerland: Certificate of civil status / Personenstandsausweis / Certificat inidividuel

Swiss nationals order the civil status certificate from the civil registry office in their place of origin. Foreign nationals at the civil registry office in their place of residence. Check out this [link](https://www.e-service.admin.ch/competency-app/wicket/bookmarkable/ch.glue.suis.competency.app.pages.HeimatortPage?0) to determine your responsible office and check their process on their website or contact them via email. They have a very fast response time (1-3 days from my experience).

The following options are available for invoicing at the office in Bern (may be different for other cantons):

**1\. A contact person with an address in Switzerland**

They can send the ordered document and the invoice to the address of this contact person in the following days. However, the law stipulates that each person can only obtain civil registration documents for themselves. Therefore they are only allowed to send them to your personal private address **. Your signature is required for delivery to the contact address (e.g. c/o address, business address, etc.).** A mail signature is not sufficient. Therefore, send your order with your own signature by post, fax or scan.

**2\. The document is sent abroad**

You will receive an invoice from them at your address or email address. As soon as they have received the amount, they will send you the document you have ordered. 

\*Pay attention – This process can take 2 to 4 weeks.

**Cost: 30CHF**

[**Link**](https://www.zivilstand.sid.be.ch/de/start/dienstleistungen/bestellung-dokumente/personenstand.html) (for Bern)

They can issue the apostille at a total cost of **97CHF**. The document is in 5 languages, French/German/Italian/English and Spanish.

## Certificate of Non-Criminal Record from Switzerland

Name in Switzerland: Criminal records excerpt / Strafregisterauszug / extrait du casier judiciaire

**How to obtain?**

From the Federal Office of Justice. Order it online or physically at any branch of the Swiss post.

**Cost: 20CHF**

Link: [**Order online**](https://www.e-service.admin.ch/crex/cms/content/strafregister/uebersicht_en)

## Verifying the documents with apostille

All official documentation from abroad that needs to be presented in Israel, must be verified by the country of its origin in order to be valid outside of it, in Israel. Many countries use “apostille” for this purpose. An apostille is usually a document, issued by an authorized authority which authenticates the documents in question.  This is used to demonstrate that they are valid documents that can be recognized in other countries.

### Obtaining apostille in Switzerland

**How to obtain?**

At your cantonal chancellery (Staatskanzelei) or at the federal chancellery in Bern – it can be requested by post or on the spot.

**Cost: 25CHF**

**[Link](https://www.sta.be.ch/en/start/dienstleistungen/dokumente-beglaubigen/vorgehen-beglaubigung.html)** (for Bern)
