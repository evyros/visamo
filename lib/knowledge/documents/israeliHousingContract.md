# Lease or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home (חוזה שכירות או רכישה של הדירה של בן/בת הזוג הישראלי/ת)

The lease or purchase contract of the Israeli partner's home, in their name: the original, a copy and the lease appendix.

For a couple who have never lived together, when the Israeli partner lives
in Israel: the lease of the Israeli partner's own home, in their name, with
all its appendices. If the Israeli partner lives abroad too, it isn't asked
for: see what to bring instead in [documents.md](../documents.md).

## What it should include

- **The Israeli partner's name on the lease.**
- **All its appendices**, with the original and a copy.
- **If the Israeli partner owns the home:** the purchase contract of the
  property, instead of a lease.

## If you don't have it

**If the Israeli partner lives in a home that isn't theirs** (for example,
their family's home, parents' home), that's possible too. Without a standard lease, the clerks look more closely at how you live, to make sure the relationship is
genuine and not only for the status. To show you have a stable, real place
to live together, bring:

- **A letter from the owners** (for example, the parents), signed: that they
  own or rent the home, that they fully support your relationship, and that they
  invite both of you to live with them long-term.
- **Their Teudat Zehut** (תעודת זהות), copies, with the appendix (ספח)
  showing their registered address.
- **Proof of the home:** the land registry extract (נסח טאבו), the purchase
  contract, or their current lease.
- **The home's bills:** recent electricity, water or arnona bills in their
  names, to show it's an active household.
- **Proof that the Israeli partner lives there:** official mail, bank
  statements or phone bills addressed to them at that address.

## Questions

**I live with my parents. Can we still apply?**
Yes, but expect the clerks to look more closely at how you live. Bring your parents' signed letter inviting you both to live with them,
copies of their Teudat Zehut with the appendix, proof of the home (the נסח טאבו the purchase contract or their lease), recent bills in their names,
and mail or statements addressed to you there.

**My partner is still abroad, but the office asked for a joint lease and shared bills. What do we send?**
Clerks sometimes work from the default list. The Israeli partner sends a
letter explaining that the foreign partner is applying from abroad, with the
lease and bills of the Israeli partner's own home.
