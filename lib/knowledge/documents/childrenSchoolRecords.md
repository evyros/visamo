# Children's school records (אישורי לימודים של הילדים)

For each child in school in Israel from age 6: a school confirmation and report cards.

The AS/6 checklist asks for school confirmations and report cards for
children in the education system from age 6. They're evidence that your
household lives in Israel, so they're for **the children living with you in
Israel**: your children together, and the foreign partner's children once
they're in school here (see [children.md](children.md)). The office may also
check with the Ministry of Education as part of the center-of-life check
(§ה.2(7)).
