# Lease or purchase contract (חוזה שכירות או רכישה)

The lease of your home in both your names, with all its appendices: the original and a copy. If one of you owns the home: its purchase contract.

For a couple who live together, in Israel or abroad: the lease of the home
you share, in both your names, with all its appendices. If you own the home,
the purchase contract instead. If you've never lived together, the Israeli
partner brings the contract of their own home instead (the lease or purchase
contract of the Israeli partner's home).

## What it should include

- **Both your names on the lease.**
- **All its appendices**, with the original and a copy.
- **If either of you owns the home you'll live in, in Israel:** the purchase
  contract of the property, instead of a lease.
- **If you live together abroad:** the lease of the home you share there. A
  contract that isn't in Hebrew or Arabic may need a translation (see
  [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## If you don't have it

If you can't show a lease or a purchase contract, show in other ways that
you run a household together, and make a strong case for it. For example:

- shared purchases, and receipts for things you bought for the home;
- where you've stayed together: bookings and other records of the places
  you lived in or stayed at together.

## Questions

**Do we really need both our names on the lease, if we live together?**
Yes. It's a very common requirement, and sometimes it holds up the whole
process. If only one of you is on the lease, ask your landlord to sign a new
lease with both your names on it.
