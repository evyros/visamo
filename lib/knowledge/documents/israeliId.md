# Teudat Zehut with its appendix (תעודת זהות עם ספח)

The Israeli partner's ID card, with the appendix (the sefach) that lists family members.

The Israeli partner's Teudat Zehut (תעודת זהות), with its appendix (ספח)
(5.2.0008 §ד.2.ג). The appendix is the page with your registered address,
civil status and family members.

## What it should include

- **The Teudat Zehut itself**, both sides of the card, with a copy.
- **The appendix (ספח)**, up to date: with your current address and civil status.
- A driver's license isn't enough: bring the Teudat Zehut itself.

It's an Israeli document, in Hebrew: no certification or translation.

## Questions

**I have a biometric ID card, without a paper appendix. What do I bring?**
Get the appendix (ספח) separately: download it from your personal area on gov.il,
or print it at a self-service station. It's free.

**Can I bring my driver's license or passport instead?**
No. Misrad Hapnim asks for the Teudat Zehut itself, with its appendix.
