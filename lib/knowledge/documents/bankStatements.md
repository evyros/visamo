# Bank statements (דפי חשבון בנק)

Statements of a shared bank account, or of each of your personal accounts, for the last 12 months: the balance and all transactions, with proof of account ownership.

For common-law couples: bank statements, of a shared bank account or of
each of your two personal accounts. They're part of the proof of your shared
life. (Married couples bring the joint bank account confirmation from form
AS/6 instead.)

## How to get it

From your bank: download the statements from its app or website, or ask at
your branch. Ask for a confirmation of account ownership too (אישור ניהול
חשבון).

## What it should include

- **The last 12 months.** The period asked for differs between Misrad Hapnim
  branches: we recommend bringing a statement for the full 12 months.
- **The balance, and all the incoming and outgoing transactions.**
- **Proof of account ownership,** with the statements.
- **For an account abroad:** a certified translation unless it's in Hebrew
  or Arabic (see [certification.md](../certification.md)). In practice,
  English is usually accepted without one.
