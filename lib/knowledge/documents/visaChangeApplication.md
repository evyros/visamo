# Visa change application (form AS/3) (בקשה להחלפת סוג אשרה (טופס אש/3))

For a foreign partner already in Israel: the application to change their visa category, filled in Hebrew and English, with a recent photo.

Form **AS/3** (אש/3), "Application for the extension of permit of residence
/ change of visa category" (בקשה להארכת רשיון ישיבה / להחלפת סוג האשרה), for
a foreign partner who is already in Israel. It's the request to change their
current visa to the one the process gives. A foreign partner who is outside
Israel files the entry permit application (form AS/1) instead.

## How to get it

The form is on the Population and Immigration Authority's site (see
[sources.md](../sources.md)).

1. Mark **change of visa category** (בקשה להחלפת סוג האשרה), and write the
   visa you're asking for.
2. Fill in the details **in Hebrew and in English**, as the form asks.
3. Attach a **recent photo**.
4. The foreign partner signs the declaration, with the place and the date.

## What it should include

- **The foreign partner's details,** in Hebrew and English: family and first
  name, previous names, civil status, country and date of birth,
  nationality, religion and citizenship; father's and mother's names and
  dates of birth, and the mother's maiden name; email.
- **The travel document:** a passport or a laissez-passer, with its number,
  issue date and expiry.
- **Their permanent address abroad,** and **their address in Israel,** each
  with a phone number.
- **The spouse's details** (פרטים של בן/בת הזוג): the Israeli partner's name,
  ID number and citizenship, and their status in Israel.
- **Children under 18,** if any: their names, country and date of birth.
  Each family member who applies needs their own form.
- **The declaration,** signed: that the details are true, that they haven't
  committed a criminal offense or acted against the Jewish people or the
  security of Israel, have no disease that endangers public health, aren't
  wanted by any police, and will keep the Authority updated on their address.
  The Authority may check further, including a home visit.
- **A recent photo,** in its box.
- **The office's part** (לשימוש המשרד): leave it empty.

The section about an educational institution is for students: leave it
empty.
