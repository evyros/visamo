# Proof of government services (אישורים על שירותים ממשלתיים)

Confirmations of services you receive in Israel, such as National Insurance (Bituach Leumi - ביטוח לאומי), health and education.

Confirmations of the services you receive from government institutions in
Israel (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ח), such as National Insurance (Bituach Leumi),
health and education (the AS/6 checklist). They're part of the proof that
your center of life is in Israel, for at least the last year. They're asked
for when at least one of you lives in Israel.

## How to get it

- **National Insurance (ביטוח לאומי):** a confirmation of insurance periods
  and employers (אישור תקופות ביטוח ומעסיקים). Download it from your
  personal area on the National Insurance site or app, or print it at a
  self-service kiosk at a branch.
- **Health:** a health fund membership confirmation (אישור חברות בקופת
  חולים), showing your health fund and the date you joined. National
  Insurance issues it too, for one person or for the whole family, from the
  same personal area.

The municipality's confirmation of your center of life (Ishur Toshav), and
the children's school records, are documents of their own.

## What it should include

- **Covering the last 12 months**, the period your center of life is proven
  for.
- **The full document**, as downloaded or printed: every page.

They're Israeli documents, in Hebrew: no certification or translation.

## Questions

**Which services count?**
The services you receive from government institutions in Israel. The AS/6
checklist gives National Insurance, health and education as examples: bring
the confirmations of the ones you receive.
