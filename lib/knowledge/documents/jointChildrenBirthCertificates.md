# Birth certificates of your children together (תעודות לידה של הילדים המשותפים)

One for each child you have together.


A birth certificate for each child you have together, listing both of you
as the parents. It's part of the proof of your relationship: the procedure
asks for any documentation that supports your relationship and shows how
long it's lasted (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ח).

## How to get it

### A child born in Israel

From the Population and Immigration Authority, free (תעודת לידה):

- **Online,** a digitally signed certificate, right away, from your personal
  area on the government's site (gov.il).
- **At a Population and Immigration Authority office,** or by mail or
  email.

It's an Israeli document: no certification or translation.

### A child born abroad

From the civil registry of the country where the child was born, like the
foreign partner's own birth certificate. It's certified there: an
apostille, or consular legalization if that country isn't in the Apostille
Convention (see [certification.md](../certification.md)).

## What it should include

- **One for each child** you have together.
- **Both of you named as the parents.**
- **For a child born abroad:** the certification of that country, and a
  certified translation unless it's in Hebrew or Arabic. In practice, an
  English document is usually accepted without one.
