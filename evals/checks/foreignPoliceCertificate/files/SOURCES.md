# Where these files come from

estonia-real.pdf is a real file, shared by the app's owner: an Estonian
Criminal Records Database notice with a notary's apostille, and an English
translation by a sworn translator in Estonia, bound together.

The Spanish files are made up for the evals, for a made-up person, in the
usual format: a Ministry of Justice criminal record certificate (certificado
de antecedentes penales), its apostille (in Spanish only), and a Hebrew
translation certified by a made-up notary in Israel. In
spain-translated.pdf the translation covers the apostille too; in
spain-apostille-not-translated.pdf it covers only the certificate. Dated
September 2026, within 6 months of the cases' date (2026-11-14).
