# Passport-size photos of the Israeli partner (תמונות פספורט של בן/בת הזוג הישראלי/ת)

Printed photos of the face, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, as in a passport: not a copy of the passport itself. Misrad Hapnim expects you to bring 3 printed copies of the same recent photo.

3 copies of the same recent passport-format photo of the Israeli partner. The
procedure asks for two (5.2.0008 §ד.2.ב), and the AS/6 checklist for 3:
bring 3.

## What it should include

The Population and Immigration Authority's format for passport photos:

- **Size:** 3.5 × 4.5 cm.
- **In color**, and recent: taken in the last few months.
- **A light, plain background** (white is best), with no shadows.
- **The face straight to the camera:** head upright, eyes open, mouth
  closed, a neutral expression.
- **The same photo** on all 3 copies.

## Questions

**Is this a copy of the passport?**
No. These are photos of the face in the format used for passports, printed
at a photo shop or photo booth, not a scan or photo of a passport's pages.

**What do we upload?**
A scan of the printed photo, or the digital file the photo shop gives you:
either is fine, as long as it's in the passport format. Bring the printed
copies to the appointment.
