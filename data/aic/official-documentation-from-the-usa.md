# Official Documentation from the United States

This post was written by a volunteer community member. While our volunteers do their best to share the most accurate information, there still may be errors or inaccurate details in this post. Please keep that in mind while reading!

This post includes detailed instructions on how to obtain and authenticate the official documentation and certifications from abroad, that are needed to apply for a partner or spouse visa in Israel. The basic personal documents you will be looking for are birth certificate, certificate of non-criminal record, current and/or previous personal status certificate (martial status), and name change certificate. Complete documentation about the foreign partner from their home country is prerequisite of the gradual process, and without it one cannot start the partner visa process and get a status in Israel based on their relationship.

**All official documentation issued abroad must be authenticated in its country of origin.** In the US, the authentication is done by an apostille. An apostille is an attached document that is used to demonstrate the original document’s validity in order to be recognized in other countries.

Because of the nature of the United States, the procedure to obtain the necessary documents may vary greatly between the states. The author of this post completed this process in Georgia and Virginia. Please refer to the Google Sheet above to ensure you are following the correct process for your state. **Go to this** [**Google Sheet**](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nynkXGGe7lccysYMeeLjKeWg0TbhBHorzfq9yVusd_U/edit?usp=sharing) **to find links relevant to your state.**

## Birth Certificate from the USA

A certified copy of your birth certificate is obtained in the **state you were born in**. In most cases,  a birth certificate is obtained from your birth state’s **Department of Health Office of Vital Records.** 

In my case, I was born in Georgia so I refer to the [Georgia Department of Health](https://dph.georgia.gov/ways-request-vital-record/birth)**.** You can request [in-person or via mail](https://dph.georgia.gov/ways-request-vital-record).

## Personal status certificate from the USA

The US doesn’t explicitly issue a personal status certificate. Instead, there is an option to issue an Affidavit of Single Status (Letter of Single Status), which is obtained in the **state you currently reside in**. It is also likely obtained via the **Department of Health Office of Vital records.**

In my case, I was living in Virginia when we started this process so I went **in-person** to the Office of Vital records in Richmond, VA. At the time, **an appointment was required.**

**Please note:** The VA Office of Vital Records had no idea what an Affidavit of Single Status was. It took some negotiating to get them to write a letter. It is a statement declaring they have NO MARRIAGE RECORD ON FILE.

In case you run into an obstacle like me, here’s a reference to my letter (printed on official letterhead):

*Dear* \[My name\],

*Reference is made to your request for a search of our marriage records.*

*A careful search of the files of the Division of Vital Records has been made. With the current indexes available, no marriage could be located for* \[my full name\] *in the Commonwealth of Virginia.*

*Sincerely,*

\[Clerk’s signature\]

\* Please note – PIBA requires to see a certificate of your personal current status. If married, you will have to show your current registered status, and proof of your previous status. If you are not single, but divorced or widowed, additional documents may be required.

## Apostille Vital Records

The above documentation should be sent to be authenticated via **Authentication Services within the issuing state** (i.e. I will send my GA birth certificate to the GA Authentication office).

In my case, I sent my Birth Certificate to the [Georgia Superior Court Clerks’ Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA)](https://www.gsccca.org/notary-and-apostilles/apostilles/general-apostille-information) and my Single Status to the [Office of the Secretary of the CommonWealth of VA](https://www.commonwealth.virginia.gov/official-documents/authentications/). Below is the process for Georgia (but they were both similar):

### Obtain an apostille for vital records via mail in Georgia, USA

1. Include a check or money order
2. Include a cover/request letter or instructional note specifying the destination country and providing contact information
3. Include a **pre-paid, self-addressed envelope** or airbill for return. 
4. Mailing Address:  
    **GSCCCA****Attn: Notary Division****1875 Century Blvd., Ste. 100****Atlanta, GA 30345**

## Certificate of non-criminal record from the USA – A Federal Background Check

The Federal Background check is conducted via the FBI. **This process is the same for ALL US citizens.** 

<table><tbody><tr><td><strong>In-person / Mail / Online</strong></td><td>Mail</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Processing time</strong></td><td>1-2 weeks via mail</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Fee</strong></td><td>$18 USD for background check + $50 USD to fingerprints via Post Office<strong> Total</strong>: $68 USD</td></tr><tr><td><a href="https://www.fbi.gov/services/cjis/identity-history-summary-checks?fbclid=IwAR1pwBGRVdJVkjSUCthGodteHAMM1OFp3QLISD_3oP30IQ-jojgDQiODZUA"><strong>Read More: FBI</strong></a><strong>&nbsp;</strong></td></tr></tbody></table>

The background check requires **fingerprints**. You can either do it from home or go to your post office. **Please Note:** FBI is incredibly picky about the fingerprints. It is recommended you do it via the Post Office rather than on your own. 

1. Fill out the request for a background check (link found [here](https://www.fbi.gov/services/cjis/identity-history-summary-checks?fbclid=IwAR1pwBGRVdJVkjSUCthGodteHAMM1OFp3QLISD_3oP30IQ-jojgDQiODZUA#:~:text=Step%201%3A%20Complete%20the%20Applicant%20Information%20Form.)).
2. [Find a post office near you](https://www.edo.cjis.gov/#/org/USPS) that takes fingerprints.
3. Submit a [payment](https://www.fbi.gov/services/cjis/identity-history-summary-checks?fbclid=IwAR1pwBGRVdJVkjSUCthGodteHAMM1OFp3QLISD_3oP30IQ-jojgDQiODZUA#:~:text=Step%203%3A%20Submit%20Payment).

### Apostille FBI Background Check

The background check must be sent for an apostille to the US Department of State.

<table><tbody><tr><td><strong>In-person / Mail / Online</strong></td><td>Mail</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Processing time</strong></td><td>10-11 weeks.&nbsp;</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Fee</strong></td><td>$20 USD per document</td></tr><tr><td><a href="https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/records-and-authentications/authenticate-your-document/requesting-authentication-services.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Read More: US Department of State</strong></a></td></tr></tbody></table>

  
**Note**: I completed this at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic so it took around 6 months for this to be returned. Currently, the US Department of State states a 10-11 weeks processing time.

1. Complete a DS-4194 (found [here](https://eforms.state.gov/Forms/ds4194.PDF) PDF)
2. Include a check or money order
3. Include the FBI Background Check
4. One self-addressed, prepaid envelope for return of your document (NOT FedEx)
5. Mailing address:

**Office of Authentications**  
**U.S. Department of State**  
**CA/PPT/S/TO/AUT**  
**44132 Mercure Circle**  
**P.O. Box 1206**  
**Sterling, VA  20166-1206**
