# Where these files come from

real-certificate.pdf and real-affidavit.pdf are real, shared by the app's
owner: a Cook County "record not found" for marriage, with its Illinois
apostille and a translation certified in Israel; and an affidavit of civil
status and no children, signed before a notary in Israel.
certificate-no-apostille.pdf and affidavit-not-notarized.pdf are their pages
(200 dpi), without the apostille, and without the notary's page and stamp.

The other countries' documents are made up for the evals, for made-up
people, in each country's usual format: no real copies of these documents
are free to use. germany-single-status.pdf (a Standesamt confirmation of
single status, with an apostille), philippines-cenomar.pdf (a PSA
certificate of no marriage record, with an apostille),
ukraine-no-marriage-record.pdf (a DRACS extract with a Hebrew translation,
without an apostille), us-no-marriage-record.pdf (a county clerk's
certification of no marriage record, with an apostille), and an affidavit of no children for each person,
before a made-up notary in Israel.

The made-up documents are dated August–September 2026, within 6 months of
the cases' fixed date (2026-10-01). The real file's are older, as they are.
